# Forza Horizon 6 — Racing, Street Racing, Touge, Drag and Time Attack

The event types you race in Forza Horizon 6: festival races, Street Races,
Touge Battles, Drag Meets, Time Attack Circuits and Rivals.

=== Racing ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Events
What racing events are there? Official events of the Horizon Festival
include Road, Dirt and Cross Country Races, each with specialised car
themes, plus Time Attack Circuits, Drag Meets, PR Stunts and Bonus Boards.
Road races stay on paved circuits and sprints, Dirt races use gravel and
trail routes, and Cross Country races cut through rough terrain across
biomes. Every event publishes a car class restriction (D, C, B, A, S1, S2 or
R), and your Wristband controls which invitations you receive. After you
beat an event once, the Race Customizer lets you replay it with any car in
your garage. Festival Playlist Championships add themed multi-race events
such as "Retro Hot Hatch - C Class" or "Modern Supercars - S1 Class".

=== Street Racing ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Events
How does street racing work? Street Races are documented events set on road
routes with a street character — examples from the Festival Playlist include
Nachi Run, River Descent, Daikoku Chase, Tokyo City Docks Charge, Kita Ine
and Matsumi Climb. They run under car themes such as Modern Super Saloons,
Retro Sports Cars and 1980s Retro Super Saloons with class restrictions
(A Class, B Class, C Class in documented examples). Street racing is also
part of how the game represents Japanese car culture — urban street racing
and Daikoku car meets were called out by the developers as core to the
setting. Fast cars with strong braking (see the street racing
recommendations) suit the mix of speed and tight urban corners.

=== Touge Battles ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Events
What are Touge Battles? Touge Battles are a new Forza Horizon 6 event type
where you race down a Japanese hillside at night — mountain-pass racing
inspired by Japan's touge culture and titles like Initial D. Touge races
also feed your Discover Japan progression: completing Street and Touge
Races earns Stamps in your Collection Journal, and Touge shows up in
Festival Playlist Weekly Challenges too. In the multiplayer suite there is
also Touge Showdown, a Horizon Play mode that runs two-player head-to-head
championships of three races. Touge and mountain routes favour cars with
exceptional handling and braking — the S15 Silvia WTAC is the documented
standout, and routes like the Hokubu Time Circuit in the mountain valleys
capture that same communal time-trial spirit.

=== Drag Meets ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Events
How do I participate in Drag Meets? Drag Meets are open-world events in Forza
Horizon 6 — you drive to a Drag Meet location and line up with other
players. They hold up to twelve players, use a synchronized start tree, and
feature in-game leaderboards. A documented Drag Meet is located at the
Urakawa Space Center, and Drag Meets also appear in the Festival Playlist
Horizon Life schedule (for example the Irokawa Quarter Mile with an S1 class
target of 11.000 seconds, and the Ito Half Mile). Drag Meets are one of the
festival's official activities, and short drag-style events also appear as
race types and time trials. Bring a car with strong Launch and Acceleration
— the documented drag specialists are the Nissan GT-R R35 Forza Edition and
the Toyota Tacoma TRD Pro Forza Edition.

=== Time Attack ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Events
How does Time Attack work? Time Attack circuits are open-world events where
you compete to set the best time on a race circuit. Their leaderboards are
filtered based on your class, and they are not permanent — they reset every
season. Time Attack Circuits are also one of the official festival event
types you use to earn Wristband progress. Documented examples include the
Hokobu Time Circuit — a mountain-valley Time Attack mission the designers
called a homage to real Japanese car culture, where the goal is simply to
set your best time and challenge your friends — plus Soni Circuit and
Sekibe Time Attack in the Festival Playlist, with class and time targets
such as "Japan - A Class - 1:06.000". The Premium Edition also includes a
Time Attack Car Pack.

=== Rivals ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Events
How do Rivals events work? In Rivals you race against a ghost to beat its
time — documented daily challenges ask you to "beat a Rivals Ghost" in
specific conditions, such as in any Japanese car, or in a car from the
1980s. Rivals is one of the ongoing activities called out as remaining
content for players to dig into after reaching the Gold Wristband.

=== PR Stunts ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Events
What PR stunts are in the game? Documented PR stunt types are Speed Traps,
Speed Zones, Danger Signs, Trailblazers and Drift Zones, each with a class
restriction and a star rating or score target — for example "Bamboo Hilltop
Speed Trap, A Class, 110.0 mph", "Airfield Takeoff Danger Sign, 698.8 feet"
and "Seaside Trail Drift Zone, 85,000 points". Earning 3 stars at
Trailblazers and Danger Signs is a recurring festival challenge, and bonus
boards (XP boards) are scattered around the world — one is hidden behind
Nachi Falls.

=== Showcases and Rush Events ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Events
What are the special events? Two special event types gate your Wristband
progress: Showcase Events — the classic cinematic Horizon races, such as
"Mech My Day" where an Acura NSX races the in-game mecha Chaser Zero — and
Horizon Rush, new obstacle challenges designed to be played over and over
where you show off your skills. Both must be completed to obtain your next
Wristband.
