# Forza Horizon 6 — EventLab and Horizon CoLab

Building and sharing custom events, and the new multiplayer build mode.

=== EventLab ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Creation
What is EventLab? EventLab is the custom-event creation suite, returning
from Forza Horizon 5 with major improvements. In Forza Horizon 6 you can
start building from anywhere on the map — create with unprecedented freedom
anywhere in Japan — and share your finished creations for everyone to play.
The game ships an always-evolving catalog of exciting new content created
by the community, and festival challenges regularly ask you to play an
EventLab event ("Community Made" is a documented daily challenge).

=== How Do I Use EventLab ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Creation
How do I use EventLab? The documented flow:
1. Enter EventLab from the game menus — you can start a build from anywhere
   in the world rather than from a fixed hub.
2. Build your custom event: routes, settings and structure, solo or with
   friends via CoLab.
3. Share the creation so other players can find and play it.
4. Browse the always-updating catalog of community events to play other
   people's work — playing one counts toward festival challenges.
EventLab creations are separate from matchmaking: custom races you want to
play with chosen rules against random opponents are handled by Custom
Racing in Horizon Play, while EventLab is for community-built content.

=== Horizon CoLab ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Creation
How does CoLab work? Horizon CoLab is the upgraded EventLab toolset, now
featuring multiplayer support and the ability to build literally anywhere
in the world. Up to 12 players can join the creator and assist with
building in a "Horizon Co-LAB" — you and your friends build custom events
together in real time, then share the result for everyone to play and
discover. Documented as "build together, anywhere in Japan": CoLab is the
social evolution of Forza's event editor, aimed at letting the community
craft the game's content collaboratively instead of solo.

=== Sharing Community Creations ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Creation
How do shared creations work? Everything made in EventLab and CoLab feeds
an always-evolving catalog of community content that all players can
discover and play. Sharing also extends beyond events: players share liveries
and tunes (visible at Car Meets and in the tune/livery browsers), and
customized garage layouts can be downloaded from the community in the same
way as EventLab creations.
