# Forza Horizon 6 — Multiplayer, Convoys and Horizon Play

Playing with friends: cross-play, Convoys, Shared World Meetups, and the
Horizon Play modes including The Eliminator, Hide & Seek and Spec Racing.

=== Multiplayer ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Multiplayer
What multiplayer options does Forza Horizon 6 have? The festival is designed
to be played with friends: you can drive together on the map, play Horizon
Festival and Discover Japan events in co-op, join Shared World Meetups,
earn cooperative LINK skills, build custom races together in CoLab, and
compete in the Horizon Play suite of online modes (The Eliminator, Hide &
Seek, Touge Showdown, Spec Racing, Horizon Racing and Horizon Drift).
Cross-play and cross-save are supported between PC and Xbox, with cross-save
also covering Steam and PlayStation 5. Online console multiplayer requires a
Game Pass subscription that includes online multiplayer.

=== Convoys ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Multiplayer
How do I join a Convoy? A Convoy is your group of friends driving together
in the open world. Documented steps:
1. Open the pause menu.
2. Navigate to the Online tab.
3. Select Convoy, then Create Convoy.
4. Invite your friends from your friends list — they accept the invitation
   and appear in your Convoy.
Alternatively, if someone invites you, click the notification saying you
have been invited and agree to the invitation to join their Convoy. On PC
(Steam) you can also open the overlay friends list (Shift+Tab), right-click
a friend who is in a Convoy and choose Join Game — returning to free roam
makes you a member of that Convoy. Once you are in a Convoy you can drive
the map together, enter events together and visit Car Meets as a group.

=== Playing with Friends ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Multiplayer
How do I play with friends? Everything social runs through Convoys and the
friends list: create a Convoy from the pause menu's Online tab and invite
people, or accept their invite. Friends in your Convoy can roam Japan with
you, join the same events, and you can watch or join each other's sessions.
Players can also send each other Gift Drops (free cars from your
collection), and community features like shared tunes, liveries, garage
layouts and EventLab creations let you play asynchronously. If Convoy
options are unavailable, privacy and network settings are the documented
things to check (online privacy permissions and NAT type).

=== Shared World Meetups ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Multiplayer
What are Shared World Meetups? Shared World Meetups are drop-in shared
sessions where players come together in the open world as part of Horizon
Play's cooperative side — listed alongside driving together, co-op LINK
skills and co-op campaign events as one of the documented ways to enjoy
Forza Horizon 6 with others.

=== Horizon Play ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Multiplayer
What is Horizon Play? Horizon Play is the suite of game modes designed to
be fun and competitive:
- The Eliminator — the open-world battle royale mode, up to 72 players.
- Hide & Seek — a game of chases where the Hider must stay hidden from
  their opponents (a documented 5 vs 1 challenge).
- Touge Showdown — two-player head-to-head championships with three races.
- Spec Racing — everyone races stock cars and the only differentiator is
  pure skill.
- Horizon Racing and Horizon Drift — standard free-for-all races and drift
  events.
- Custom Racing — matchmaking where you set the car class and race type.
- Custom Drifting — choose the car class and drive type.
Playing these earns XP towards Horizon Play level 100, Badges every 10
ranks, and (up to level 25) Horizon Festival Points towards your Wristbands.

=== The Eliminator ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Multiplayer
How does The Eliminator work? The Eliminator is the open-world battle royale
mode that first appeared in Forza Horizon 4 and returns in Forza Horizon 6
for up to 72 players. You start in the open world and face off against the
other players — documented Festival Playlist challenges include finishing
30th or better and completing head-to-head battles. It runs as part of
Horizon Play, so playing it also earns Horizon Play XP and ranks.

=== Hide & Seek ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Multiplayer
How does Hide & Seek work? Hide & Seek is a returning Horizon multiplayer
mode described as a game of chases: the Hider must stay hidden from their
opponents. Documented Festival Playlist challenges run it as "5 vs 1" —
complete a game of Hide & Seek as one of the Horizon Play activities. It is
part of the Horizon Play suite, so it also earns Horizon Play progression.

=== Spec Racing ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Multiplayer
How does Spec Racing work? Spec Racing is a new Forza Horizon 6 mode where
everyone races stock cars and the only differentiator is pure skill. The
campaign side adds Spec Racing Championships as a new event type, and
Horizon Play hosts Spec Racing with its own challenges (documented example:
earn 3 Ultimate Clean Racing Skills in Spec Racing). Because the cars are
standardised, it is the mode that most directly rewards driving ability.

=== The Trial ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Multiplayer
What is The Trial? The Trial is a documented Horizon Life event in the
Festival Playlist — a team-based championship against tough opposition, with
a car reward (for example the 1986 Audi #2 Audi Sport quattro S1 for the
"Push it to the Limit" 1980s Total Rally B-class Trial). It runs on dirt
routes in the documented example and, like other Horizon Life content, sits
alongside Rivals and Time Attack in the playlist's line-up.
