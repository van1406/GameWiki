# Forza Horizon 6 — Upgrades

What the Upgrade Shop covers, how conversions work, and how upgrade choices
interact with the Performance Index.

=== Upgrades ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Tuning
How do upgrades work? Upgrades are the parts you buy and install in the
Upgrade Shop to make a car faster, lighter or more adjustable. Every part
costs Performance Index, so builds are a balance: spend PI on the changes
that matter for your discipline rather than maxing every category. The main
documented upgrade groups are Platform & Handling (springs and dampers,
anti-roll bars, brakes, weight reduction and chassis reinforcement/roll
cage), drivetrain parts, tires and compounds, and aerodynamic options.

=== How Do I Upgrade My Car ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Tuning
How do I upgrade my car? Steps:
1. Open the Upgrade Shop from the festival menus or your garage.
2. Pick the category that matches your goal: Platform & Handling for grip
   and balance, drivetrain for power delivery, tires for traction, and aero
   for downforce.
3. Watch the Performance Index readout — if the car crosses into the next
   class it may no longer fit the events you want, so upgrade within your
   target class.
4. Fit Race-tier parts where you want tuning sliders (Race Springs & Dampers,
   Race anti-roll bars, Race brakes).
5. Consider conversions — engine swaps, drivetrain conversions and body
   kits — when the stock engine cannot reach your target class.
6. After upgrading, open the Tune menu to set up the new parts, or download
   a community tune.
Spending 10,000 credits on upgrades for a Japanese car even counts as a
festival challenge, so upgrades are part of normal progression.

=== Upgrade Categories ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Tuning
What upgrade categories exist? Documented options include:
- Springs & Dampers (Stock, Street, Sport, Race, Rally, Off-Road) — the
  most important choice, because Race tier unlocks the full tuning suite.
- Anti-Roll Bars (Stock through Race, front and rear independently).
- Brakes (Stock, Street, Sport, Race — Race unlocks brake balance and
  pressure tuning).
- Weight Reduction (Stock, Street, Sport, Race) — strips weight for
  acceleration, braking and cornering gains.
- Chassis Reinforcement / Roll Cage (Stock through Race) — stiffer body,
  more precision, added weight; great for dirt and cross-country builds,
  usually skipped for drag builds.
- Drivetrain parts (clutch, driveline, transmission, differential) for
  power delivery and gearing.
- Tires and tire compounds for grip.
- Aerodynamics: body kits and new Forza Aero options for downforce.

=== Engine Swaps and Drivetrain Conversion ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Tuning
Can I swap engines or change drivetrain? Yes. Engine Swap and drivetrain
conversion are documented upgrade paths in Forza Horizon 6. The documented
extreme example is the 1985 Toyota Sprinter Trueno (AE86) Forza Edition,
which reaches top speeds exceeding 322 mph when fully upgraded with a 2JZ
engine swap, an all-wheel-drive conversion and a specialised drag tune.
Conversions change the car's character completely — and its Performance
Index — so check your class budget after installing one.

=== Body Kits and Forza Aero ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Tuning
What cosmetic-performance upgrades are new? Forza Horizon 6 lets your
favourite cars be upgraded with body kits and new Forza Aero options, and
adds the ability to paint custom liveries on windows. Forza Aero parts
(front and rear aero) also matter in tuning because they add adjustable
downforce — the balance between top speed on Japan's highways and grip in
the corners of Touge and circuit races.

=== Weight Reduction ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Tuning
When should I use weight reduction? Documented guidance: always on heavy
cars (trucks, luxury sedans, classic muscle) where Race tier pays for
itself; selectively on mid-weight cars where Sport tier is usually the
sweet spot; and often skipped on already-light kei cars where the PI cost
can outweigh the benefit. Weight reduction is one of the few upgrades that
improves acceleration, braking and cornering at the same time.
