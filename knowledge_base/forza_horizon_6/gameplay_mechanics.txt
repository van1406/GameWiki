# Forza Horizon 6 — Gameplay Mechanics

LINK skills, side activities and the everyday systems that run underneath
the festival.

=== Gameplay Mechanics ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Mechanics
What are the core gameplay mechanics of Forza Horizon 6? The loop is:
explore Japan and fill your Collection Journal (Stamps), race official
festival events for Horizon Festival Points (Wristbands), play Horizon Play
modes for online progression, and spend what you earn on cars, houses and
upgrades. Around that sit PR stunts, Rivals, Car Meets, EventLab/CoLab
creation, side jobs, gift-giving, photo mode and the weekly Festival
Playlist. Seasons rotate weekly and change both the world and the playlist.

=== LINK Skills ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Mechanics
What are LINK skills? LINK skills are new cooperative skills you earn with
other players — the official feature description is "earn new co-op LINK
skills with other players", and documented daily challenges simply ask you
to "earn a LINK Skill" while driving with others. They fit the social side
of Horizon Play and co-op play: doing things alongside other drivers in the
open world or in meetups. Forza LINK phrases also appear as Festival
Playlist rewards (QuickChat phrases you can equip), and QuickChat lets you
fire off messages like "Oooh… Shiny!" or "Beam me up!" while driving.

=== Raku-Raku Food Delivery Job ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Mechanics
Is there a job system? Yes — the Raku-Raku food delivery job is a
documented way to earn additional income in Forza Horizon 6, and it feeds
Discover Japan progression: earning income at the job counts towards your
next Stamp. Seasonal versions appear in the Festival Playlist, such as
"Tokyo City Food Delivery — complete a full shift", which pays out a reward
car (the 1987 Nissan Be-1 in Series 01).

=== Gift Drops ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Mechanics
How do Gift Drops work? Gift Drops let you send a car from your collection
to another player — documented daily challenges ask you to "send a Gift
Drop to another player", and some cars are handed out to the community as
Gift Cars during windows (for example the 1987 Nissan Be-1 for players who
played between 28 May and 18 June). It is a free way to share your spare
cars with friends or strangers.

=== Photo Mode and Murals ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Mechanics
How does photography work? The photo feature lets you capture points of
interest, iconic landmarks and natural wonders for your Journal, and photos
are also challenges: Festival Playlist Photo Challenges ask you to
photograph specific spots (#TokyoTower, #FestivalVibes at the Horizon
Festival Japan Site, #AstroToy at the Space Port) or specific cars. Murals
by real artists (Kenji Iwasaki, Okuyama Taiki, Kazuhisa Uragami, Wakana
Yamazaki, Tatsuro Kiuchi, Naoshi, Lady Aiko, Dragon76, Inko Takita) are
scattered across the world — photographing them counts towards Stamps.

=== Barn Finds ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Mechanics
How do Barn Finds work? Collecting Stamps for your Collection Journal
unlocks Barn Find Rumors — hints that send you hunting for abandoned cars
around Japan. Documented example: the 1991 Mazda #55 Mazda 787B is a barn
find, and it is also referenced as a car suited to the R-class Goliath.
Barn finds are a cheap way to add rare machinery to your collection.

=== PR Stunts and Bonus Boards ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Mechanics
How do PR stunts and bonus boards work? Speed Traps, Speed Zones, Danger
Signs, Trailblazer and Drift Zone events are scattered across the map with
class restrictions and star ratings — earn 3 stars for the best rewards.
Bonus Boards (XP boards) are hidden objects you smash for experience, with
at least one deliberately hidden behind the base of Nachi Falls. PR stunts
also count towards festival events and playlist challenges.

=== Drivatars and Difficulty ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Mechanics
Who am I racing against in single player? Your AI opponents are Drivatars,
the series' learned AI drivers. After launch, players criticised Drivatar
difficulty even at lower settings, and Playground Games addressed the issue
in a post-launch update alongside other fixes — so if the AI feels too
aggressive, difficulty settings and updates are the documented answer.

=== Character Customization ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Mechanics
Can I customise my character? Yes — your character is customised and shown
off with your cars at Car Meets, where meets are documented as places to
"showcase your cars and characters". Character items also appear among the
Festival Playlist's documented reward types (alongside cars, credits,
horns, QuickChat and LINK phrases), so cosmetics for your driver are
awarded through seasonal play. The knowledge base does not have a full list
of character customization options.

=== Side Content Overview ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Mechanics
What else is there to do besides racing? Documented side content:
- Discover Japan: landmarks, murals, Day Trips, roads and regions tracked
  by the Journal's fog-of-war map.
- Car culture: Car Meets, Drag Meets, touge nights and street routes.
- Creation: EventLab and Horizon CoLab.
- Economy: food delivery jobs, Auction House trading, Gift Drops.
- Stunts: PR stunt boards, bonus boards, treasure hunts.
- Social: Convoys, LINK skills, Shared World Meetups.
- Competition: Rivals, Time Attack leaderboards, Series Standing
  Leaderboards, The Trial.
