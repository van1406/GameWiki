# Forza Horizon 6 — Game Overview

What Forza Horizon 6 is, where and when it released, what the Horizon Festival
is, and how the world of Japan is put together.

=== Game Overview ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Overview
Forza Horizon 6 (abbreviated FH6) is an open-world racing game developed by
Playground Games with assistance from Turn 10 Studios and published by Xbox
Game Studios. It is the sixth Forza Horizon title and the fourteenth main
instalment in the Forza franchise. The game is set in a fictionalised
representation of Japan and revolves around the Horizon Festival: you start
as a tourist, qualify for the festival as a rookie driver, and rise through
the ranks by racing in progressively faster cars until you become a Horizon
Legend. It launched with over 550 real-world cars, a campaign playable solo
or in co-op with friends, and a large suite of multiplayer modes.

=== Horizon Festival ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Overview
The Horizon Festival is the travelling racing festival at the heart of
Forza Horizon 6, and this time it is held in Japan. You begin the game as a
tourist visiting Japan, not as a festival driver: you must first prove you
have what it takes by completing the Horizon Qualifiers and the Horizon
Invitational to be accepted as a rookie driver. Official events of the
Horizon Festival include Road, Dirt and Cross Country Races with specialised
car themes, plus Time Attack Circuits, Drag Meets, PR Stunts and Bonus
Boards. As you earn Wristbands you are invited to events with higher car
class restrictions. The festival has a main site — the Horizon Festival
Japan Site — and a second Horizon Festival Site that opens on Legend Island
once you reach Horizon Legend status. The story also introduces you to the
Legends of the Horizon Festival, the veteran drivers whose careers you follow.

=== The Japan Setting ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Overview
Forza Horizon 6 is set in Japan, with Mount Fuji and Tokyo confirmed
locations, and the game world is described as the franchise's largest map.
It is not a one-to-one recreation of the country: the team aimed to capture
Japan's cultural essence, connecting real landmarks in an arrangement that
works for driving. The map contrasts dense urban Japan — Tokyo City, the
largest urban area ever in a Forza Horizon game — with rural countryside,
alpine hills, coasts and mountains. Kyoko Yamashita worked as cultural
consultant, and the team travelled to Japan for location research, including
experiencing Japanese car culture first hand. Seasonal changes matter:
cherry blossom blooms, heavy snowfall and other seasonal shifts change how
the world looks and drives.

=== Release Date and Platforms ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Overview
Forza Horizon 6 was announced at the 2025 Tokyo Game Show on 25 September
2025 and released on 19 May 2026 for Xbox Series X|S, Windows PC (Microsoft
Store and Steam) and Xbox Cloud Gaming, as an Xbox Play Anywhere title. It
was playable day one with Xbox Game Pass (Game Pass Ultimate and PC Game
Pass). Buyers of the Premium Edition got four days of early access starting
15 May 2026. A PlayStation 5 release is planned for later in 2026. Cross-play
and cross-save are supported between PC and Xbox, and cross-save also works
between Xbox, Steam and PlayStation 5.

=== Development ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Overview
Forza Horizon 6 was developed by Playground Games in Leamington Spa with
assistance from Turn 10 Studios, which paused its work on Forza Motorsport to
focus on helping polish FH6. The game runs on the ForzaTech engine, is the
first Forza Horizon title built only for ninth-generation hardware (Xbox One
support was dropped), and introduces ray-traced global illumination on PC
alongside traditional lighting. On Xbox consoles players can choose a Quality
mode (30 fps) or a Performance mode (60 fps).

=== Single-Player and Co-op ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Overview
The campaign can be played entirely solo, and most of it can also be played
in co-op with friends: Horizon Festival and Discover Japan events support
co-op play, you can drive together around the open world, earn cooperative
LINK skills, take part in Shared World Meetups, and build custom races
together in CoLab. Online console multiplayer requires a Game Pass
subscription that includes online multiplayer.

=== Discover Japan and the Journal ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Overview
Exploration is a core part of the game. The Journal feature encourages you
to document the world: a fog-of-war layer on the map shows where you have
been and where you have yet to visit, and a photography feature lets you
capture points of interest, iconic landmarks and natural wonders for your
Journal. Discovering things across Japan — landmarks, murals, cars and more —
progresses your Collection Journal and earns Stamps. Day Trip missions are
bite-sized activities scattered around the map, inspired by the trips real
visitors to Japan often take, with a Horizon twist.
