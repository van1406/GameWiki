# Forza Horizon 6 — Car Recommendations by Category

Best-car recommendations for common questions — sedans, SUVs, sports cars,
supercars, hypercars, JDM, muscle, drift, rally, off-road, drag, street,
beginners, AWD, RWD, Tokyo City, Touge and value. Based only on documented
Forza Horizon 6 information; nothing here is an objective ranking.

=== How Recommendations Work ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Recommendations
How should I read these recommendations? Forza Horizon 6 has over 550 cars
and no single car is objectively best for everything — different cars suit
different classes, surfaces, drivetrains and driver skill levels. These
recommendations follow four rules:
1. Every pick is based on documented performance, handling, acceleration,
   braking, top speed, drivetrain, class or intended use — never invented
   statistics.
2. Stock performance (straight from the Autoshow or a reward) and tuned
   performance (after upgrades and tuning) are described separately, and
   where a car only shines after tuning, that is stated.
3. Where several cars are strong, several options are given with the
   differences explained, instead of one "winner".
4. Where the knowledge base does not have enough information to support a
   category, it says so rather than inventing an answer.
This file is meant to be updated as new cars and content are added.

=== Best Sedans ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Recommendations
What is the best sedan in Forza Horizon 6? The knowledge base does not
document a single official "best sedan", so here are the documented strong
options instead:
Car: 2004 Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution VIII MR
Manufacturer: Mitsubishi
Type: rally-bred performance sedan
Drivetrain: all-wheel drive
Why it is recommended: documented as the best rally car in the game, with
slightly stronger speed, offroad and handling stats than other cars in its
category.
Best use: dirt and rally events, all-weather street driving.
Stock performance: Speed 6.9, Handling 6.5, Acceleration 7.7, Launch 4.1,
Braking 5.1, Offroad 6.9 — competitive without any work.
Tuned performance: additional upgrades make it far stronger and more
competitive, and it is cheap to upgrade.
How to obtain: cheap from the Autoshow; also the Auction House and
Wheelspins.
Car: 2004 Subaru Impreza WRX STI
Manufacturer: Subaru
Type: performance sedan
Drivetrain: all-wheel drive
Why it is recommended: a solid, cheap option that after upgrades handles
offroad and rally races better than most other vehicles.
Best use: rally, dirt and mixed conditions on a budget.
Stock performance: documented as not the best out of the box (Speed 5.9,
Handling 4.9, Acceleration 6.0, Offroad 5.9).
Tuned performance: cheap to upgrade; turns into a strong competitor.
How to obtain: Autoshow, Wheelspin, Auction House, or as an Aftermarket Car.

Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Recommendations
What is the best car for Tokyo City? Tokyo City is the largest urban area in
Forza Horizon 6, mixing dense districts, docks and an elevated highway loop,
so the best answer depends on where you drive:
For the districts (Shibuya Crossing, Akihabara, Daikoku):
Car: 1993 Autozam AZ-1
Manufacturer: Autozam (Mazda)
Type: kei sports car
Drivetrain: rear-wheel drive
Why it is recommended: its tiny kei-car dimensions suit Tokyo's densest
streets and narrow corners, and it is a documented Festival Playlist car
(the "Lookin So Cute!" challenge asks you to park it at any Car Meet).
Best use: city cruising, car meets, tight urban sections.
Stock performance: not documented in this knowledge base.
How to obtain: Festival Playlist reward / documented in challenges.
Car: 1994 Mazda MX-5 Forza Edition
Manufacturer: Mazda
Type: lightweight roadster
Drivetrain: rear-wheel drive
Why it is recommended: documented as a drift-zone and city favourite that is
commonly found as an Aftermarket Car near city-based events, with superb
braking for stop-start urban driving.
Best use: drifting at Shibuya Crossing, city streets.
Stock performance: Launch 10, Braking 9.8, Handling 7.9.
How to obtain: Festival Playlist, or Aftermarket Car near city events.
For the highways and docks (high-speed runs, Daikoku Chase, the Highway
Loop):
Car: 2012 Nissan GT-R Black Edition R35 Forza Edition
Drivetrain: all-wheel drive
Why it is recommended: 305 mph top speed and Acceleration 10 for the
elevated highway loop.
Best use: highway speed runs, drag events at the docks.
Car: 2021 Mercedes-AMG One
Drivetrain: rear-wheel drive
Why it is recommended: Acceleration 10 — documented as able to blaze across
Japan's highways in seconds.
Best use: long highway sprints.
For street-race events inside the city, the documented community pick is
the Toyota GR86 (see Best Street Racing Cars).

=== Best Cars for Touge ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Recommendations
What is the best car for Touge, mountain roads and night hill climbs? Touge
Battles are races down a Japanese hillside at night, and mountain roads
like Mount Haruna's winding climbs demand handling above all:
Car: 2000 Nissan #36 Dream Project S15 Silvia WTAC
Manufacturer: Nissan
Type: time-attack tuned Silvia
Drivetrain: rear-wheel drive
Why it is recommended: documented as by far the best handling car in the
game — at max power with upgrades it handles tight corners and Touge racing
better and more consistently than anything else.
Best use: Touge Battles, twisty hillside circuits, technical road races.
Stock performance: Speed 6.7, Handling 9.3, Acceleration 7.7, Braking 10.
Tuned performance: shines brightest at max power; this is the top documented
touge pick.
How to obtain: not documented in this knowledge base.
Car: 1993 Nissan #32 Skyline WTAC 'Xtreme GTR'
Manufacturer: Nissan
Type: time-attack tuned Skyline
Drivetrain: all-wheel drive
Why it is recommended: documented alternate very similar to the Silvia but
with better offroad capabilities.
Best use: longer cross-country and endurance events — avoid short tracks,
because its Launch (2.1) is extremely weak.
Stock performance: Speed 6.8, Handling 9.6, Acceleration 7.8, Braking 10.
How to obtain: not documented in this knowledge base.
Car: 1989 Nissan Silvia K's
Manufacturer: Nissan
Type: classic rear-drive coupe
Drivetrain: rear-wheel drive
Why it is recommended: community drift/touge guidance calls the Nissan
Silvia S15 the best all-round drift and touge car for balance and throttle
control, and the Silvia family is the standard recommendation for mountain
passes; the K's is the free starter version to learn on.
Best use: learning touge and drift technique.
How to obtain: free starter car.
Backup: the 2005 Porsche Cayman GT3 WTAC (Handling 9.7, Braking 10) keeps
its speed through sharp corners and windy hills.

=== Best Value Cars ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Recommendations
What is the best car for value — strong performance for few credits? The
documented bargains:
Car: 2004 Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution VIII MR
Manufacturer: Mitsubishi
Type: rally-bred performance sedan
Drivetrain: all-wheel drive
Why it is recommended: documented as the best rally car in the game while
being cheap from the Autoshow — the strongest documented performance per
credit.
Best use: rally and dirt racing cheaply.
Stock performance: top of its category in speed, offroad and handling.
Tuned performance: cheap upgrades make it competitive far beyond its price.
How to obtain: cheap from the Autoshow, Auction House, Wheelspins.
Car: 1989 Nissan Silvia K's / 1970 GMC Jimmy / 1994 Toyota Celica GT-Four
ST205
Type: the three starter cars
Why they are recommended: they are free — you receive all three after the
introduction — and each covers a different discipline (all-round, offroad,
speed).
Best use: the entire opening of the game without spending credits.
How to obtain: free after the introduction.
Car: 2004 Subaru Impreza WRX STI
Manufacturer: Subaru
Type: performance sedan
Drivetrain: all-wheel drive
Why it is recommended: cheap to buy and cheap to upgrade, and then better
than most in rally events.
How to obtain: Autoshow, Wheelspin, Auction House, Aftermarket Car.
Free-car strategy: Festival Playlist Season Rewards cost only 15 points
(30 for the second car) each season, Series Rewards open at 60 points,
Aftermarket Cars are discounted below Autoshow prices, and loyalty rewards
from previous Forza games arrive free in your garage — documented free cars
include the Mercedes-AMG One, Lamborghini Centenario, Aston Martin Vulcan,
Lamborghini Huracan, Dodge SRT Viper GTS and Corvette E-Ray.
