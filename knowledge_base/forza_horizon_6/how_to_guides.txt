# Forza Horizon 6 — Practical How-To Guides

Direct answers to the common practical questions, phrased the way players
ask them.

=== How Do I Unlock Legend Island ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Guides
How do I unlock Legend Island? Bank 32,500 points on the Horizon Festival
progression path, which unlocks the Horizon Legend wristband event. Drive
from the airfield (the same one used for the Flight Club event) down to
Legend Island, an island directly east of Tokyo Docks — the trip is a
non-competitive celebration drive with the cast of characters, and you are
given a Lamborghini Aventador LP 780-4 Ultimate that you keep. Finish it
and you receive the Gold Wristband: Legend Island, the Legends Island
Circuit and the huge endgame races (The Goliath, Colossus, Titan and
Gauntlet) are now open to you.

=== How Do I Get a New Car ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Guides
How do I get a new car? Buy it at the Autoshow with credits, bid for it at
the Auction House, win it from races, Championships or the Festival
Playlist, roll it from a Wheelspin (available after you qualify for the
festival), find a rare Forza Edition or Aftermarket Car parked in the world
and buy it discounted, receive it as a loyalty or Gift Drop reward, or get
it weekly from the Car Pass if you own it. Your three free starter cars
come after the introduction.

=== How Do I Buy Cars ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Guides
How do I buy cars? The Autoshow is the standard way: browse the catalogue
and pay credits. The Auction House lets you bid on other players' cars,
often below Autoshow prices. Aftermarket Cars parked around the world can
be test-driven then bought at a discount compared to the Autoshow. You
also "buy" progress-based cars by spending Festival Playlist points rather
than credits.

=== How Do I Tune My Car ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Guides
How do I tune my car? Install upgrade parts that unlock tuning sliders (the
big one is Race Springs & Dampers, plus Race anti-roll bars and Race
brakes), open the Tune menu, then adjust tire pressure, gearing, alignment
(camber, toe, caster), spring rates, ride height, damping (bump/rebound),
anti-roll bar stiffness and brake balance/pressure. Test, then adjust by
feel: understeer means soften the front, oversteer means soften the rear.
If you would rather not tune yourself, download a community tune — at Car
Meets you can even take a full copy of another player's car.

=== How Do I Upgrade My Car ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Guides
How do I upgrade my car? Use the Upgrade Shop: Platform & Handling parts
(springs and dampers, anti-roll bars, brakes, weight reduction, chassis
reinforcement), drivetrain parts, tires, and aero — plus conversions
(engine swaps, drivetrain conversions, body kits). Watch the Performance
Index so you stay in the class you want to race, fit Race-tier parts where
you want tuning sliders, and remember weight reduction is the upgrade that
improves acceleration, braking and cornering at once.

=== How Do I Join a Convoy ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Guides
How do I join a Convoy? Open the pause menu, go to the Online tab, select
Convoy and Create Convoy, then invite friends from your friends list — they
accept the invitation and appear with you. Or, if you are invited, click
the invitation notification and agree to it to join theirs. On PC (Steam)
you can also Shift+Tab to the friends list, right-click a friend in a
Convoy and choose Join Game.

=== What Are Touge Battles ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Guides
What are Touge Battles? Touge Battles are new Forza Horizon 6 events where
you race down a Japanese hillside at night — mountain-pass racing inspired
by Japan's touge culture. They also feed your Collection Journal (completing
Street and Touge Races earns Stamps) and appear in Festival Playlist
challenges. For multiplayer, Touge Showdown runs two-player head-to-head
championships of three races. The documented top pick for touge is the
Nissan S15 Silvia WTAC, thanks to its outstanding handling and braking.

=== How Do I Participate in Drag Meets ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Guides
How do I participate in Drag Meets? Drive to a Drag Meet location — they
are open-world, and one is documented at the Urakawa Space Center — and
line up: Drag Meets hold up to twelve players, use a synchronized start
tree, and track in-game leaderboards. Drag Meets are also official festival
activities that earn Wristband progress, and they appear in the Festival
Playlist Horizon Life schedule with class and time targets (for example S1
class, 11.000 seconds at the Irokawa Quarter Mile). Bring a car with strong
Launch and Acceleration.

=== How Does the Festival Playlist Work ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Guides
How does the Festival Playlist work? Each season rotates a set of events
and challenges — Weekly and Daily challenges, Photo Challenges, Treasure
Hunts, Championships, PR stunt targets, Time Attack/Drag Meet targets,
Horizon Life and Horizon Play challenges. Completing them earns playlist
points; points unlock Season Rewards at low thresholds (15/30 in Series 01)
and Series Rewards at high thresholds (60/120). Rewards are exclusive to
their season, so grab them before the playlist rotates. A new Series starts
fresh (Series 01 "Welcome To Japan", then Series 02 "Horizon Decades") and
resets the series leaderboards.

=== How Do I Earn Reward Cars ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Guides
How do I earn reward cars? Push playlist points for Season and Series
rewards, win Championships for their listed car, complete Horizon Play
challenge goals, progress your Wristbands (the Porsche 911 Rallye comes
with the Orange Expert Wristband; the Aventador Ultimate comes with the
Horizon Legend event), claim loyalty cars if you played previous Forza
games, watch for community Gift Cars, and collect weekly Car Pass cars if
you own it.

=== What Is EventLab ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Guides
What is EventLab? EventLab is the custom-event creation suite: build your
own races and events from anywhere on the map, then share them in the
always-evolving catalog of community content for everyone to play. Playing
community events even counts as a festival challenge. The CoLab upgrade
adds multiplayer building — see the next question.

=== How Does CoLab Work ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Guides
How does CoLab work? Horizon CoLab is the upgraded EventLab toolset with
multiplayer support: up to 12 players can join the creator and help build
an event together — a "Horizon Co-LAB" — and you can build literally
anywhere in Japan. Finished creations are shared for everyone to discover
and play.

=== Where Is Tokyo City ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Guides
Where is Tokyo City? Tokyo City is the largest urban area ever in a Forza
Horizon game — five times bigger than previous titles' cities — and the
home for car enthusiasts on the Japan map. It runs from suburban outskirts
to neon downtown streets, the docks and industrial districts, with elevated
highways above and famous spots inside: Shibuya Crossing, Ginkgo Avenue,
Akihabara, Daikoku and Tokyo Tower. Legend Island sits directly east of the
Tokyo Docks.

=== What Is the Horizon Festival ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Guides
What is the Horizon Festival? It is the racing festival at the heart of
Forza Horizon 6, held in Japan. You start as a tourist, qualify through the
Horizon Qualifiers and Horizon Invitational, and join as a rookie driver.
Official festival events are Road, Dirt and Cross Country races plus Time
Attack Circuits, Drag Meets, PR Stunts and Bonus Boards; earning Wristbands
rises you through its ranks until you become a Horizon Legend with access
to Legend Island and its second festival site.

=== What Are Wristbands ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Guides
What are Wristbands? Wristbands are the campaign's progression ranks —
your ticket to invitations for higher car-class events at the Horizon
Festival. There are seven, from the first Yellow Wristband up to the Gold
Wristband (Horizon Legend). You earn Horizon Festival Points by racing
official events, then complete a Wristband Event — a Showcase or Horizon
Rush challenge — to claim the next band. Your first band requires
completing the Horizon Qualifiers and the Horizon Invitational.

=== How Do I Progress Through the Campaign ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Guides
How do I progress through the campaign? Qualify (Qualifiers +
Invitational), race festival events for points, complete each Wristband
Event when it unlocks, and keep climbing the seven Wristbands while
upgrading to faster cars. Collect Stamps alongside this to unlock homes,
Barn Finds and the Estate, and reach 32,500 festival points to trigger the
Horizon Legend event that opens Legend Island and the endgame races.

=== How Do Car Meets Work ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Guides
How do Car Meets work? They are open-world social spaces — three
documented locations: the Horizon Festival, the Okuibuki parking area and
Daikoku. Drive in, park up, and showcase your car and character; download
other players' Tunes, Liveries and full cars; take photos; then group up
and go driving or racing together. Drag Meets are the competitive
straight-line sibling, with twelve players, a start tree and leaderboards.

=== How Do I Customize My Garage ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Guides
How do I customize my garage? Player houses come with customizable
garages: pick which cars to display, decorate the space freely, invite
other players in to see it — or download a layout shared by the community
instead of starting from scratch. Your garages also store your cars and
double as fast travel points across Japan.

=== How Do I Get Aftermarket Cars ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Guides
How do I get Aftermarket Cars? Find them in the open world: they are rare
special cars parked at fixed set positions, placed to suit their location
— near drag strips, around city events, downstream of Nachi Falls. Test
drive before you buy; they cost less than the same car at the Autoshow, and
purchasing one even counts towards festival challenges. Some Forza Edition
cars appear the same way, such as the Mazda MX-5 Forza Edition near drag
strips and city-based events.
