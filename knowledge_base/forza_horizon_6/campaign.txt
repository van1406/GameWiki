# Forza Horizon 6 — Campaign, Events and Legend Island

How the campaign flows from tourist to Horizon Legend, what Showcase and
Horizon Rush events are, and how the endgame on Legend Island unlocks.

=== Campaign ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Campaign
How does the campaign work? The Forza Horizon 6 campaign is similar to the
original Forza Horizon: you start as a tourist exploring Japan rather than a
Horizon Superstar or rookie driver. First you complete a qualifier to join
the Horizon Festival. After that you work your way up the ranks by racing in
faster and faster cars, which earns you new Wristbands. After each round of
racing you must complete a special event — such as a Showcase event or a
Horizon Rush event — to prove you are ready for the next stage. Alongside
the racing you are free to explore Japan, fill your Collection Journal with
Stamps, and take on side activities such as Day Trips, food delivery jobs
and car meets.

=== How Do I Progress Through the Campaign ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Campaign
How do I progress through the campaign? Follow these steps:
1. Complete the Horizon Qualifiers and the Horizon Invitational to join the
   festival and earn your first Wristband.
2. Race in official festival events — Road, Dirt and Cross Country races,
   Time Attack Circuits, Drag Meets, PR Stunts and Bonus Boards — to earn
   Horizon Festival Points.
3. When enough points are banked, a Wristband Event unlocks: a Showcase
   Event or a Horizon Rush obstacle challenge. Complete it to earn the next
   Wristband and invites to faster events.
4. Repeat the loop, using the higher-class events and prize credits to buy
   and upgrade faster cars.
5. Reach 32,500 points on the Horizon Festival path to unlock the Horizon
   Legend wristband event, which takes you to Legend Island and opens the
   endgame races.
After completing a race for the first time, the Race Customizer unlocks for
that event, letting you replay it with any car in your garage.

=== Horizon Festival Events ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Campaign
What events can I play in the campaign? Official events of the Horizon
Festival include Road, Dirt and Cross Country Races, each with specialised
car themes, as well as Time Attack Circuits, Drag Meets, PR Stunts and Bonus
Boards. On top of those you will find Street Races, Touge Battles, Day Trip
missions, Showcase Events, Horizon Rush challenges, Rivals, and the seasonal
content in the Festival Playlist. Events specify a car class (for example D,
C, B, A, S1 or S2), and higher Wristbands open invites to events with higher
class restrictions.

=== Wristband Events ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Campaign
What is a Wristband Event? Once you have accumulated enough progress, a new
Wristband Event becomes available. These are the special events you must
complete to obtain your next Wristband: the classic Showcase Events and the
new Horizon Rush obstacle challenges. They act as the gate between Wristband
tiers, so when the campaign seems to stall, look for the Wristband Event
marker rather than more ordinary races.

=== Race Customizer ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Campaign
After a race has been completed for the first time, the Race Customizer
unlocks for that specific event. On subsequent replays you can use any car
in your garage (not just cars that meet the original restriction) and
customise the experience in other ways before starting the race.

=== Horizon Showcase Events ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Campaign
What is a Showcase event? Showcase Events are the large, cinematic special
races of the Horizon series, and in Forza Horizon 6 they are one of the two
types of Wristband Events you must complete to advance. A documented FH6
example is the "Mech My Day" showcase, in which an Acura NSX races against
Chaser Zero, an in-game mecha. Showcases are handed out as proof that you
are ready for the next stage of the festival.

=== Horizon Rush ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Campaign
What is Horizon Rush? Horizon Rush is a new event type in Forza Horizon 6
where you show off your skills on various obstacles — they are obstacle
challenges designed to be played over and over. Like Showcase Events,
Horizon Rush events are Wristband Events you must complete to obtain your
next Wristband, and they also appear as standalone activities — festival
challenges routinely ask you to simply "complete a Horizon Rush event".

=== Day Trips ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Campaign
Day Trip missions are bite-sized pieces of content scattered around the map,
inspired by the things visitors to Japan often do, with a Horizon twist.
They are part of the Discover Japan structure and lead you to tourist hot
spots such as Ruriko-ji Temple and Hirosaki Castle, rewarding you for
exploring.

=== Legend Island ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Campaign
What is Legend Island? Legend Island is an exclusive region of the map,
located directly east of Tokyo Docks, that opens once you earn the Gold
Wristband and reach Horizon Legend status. It is reserved for the greatest
drivers and features special events, exclusive areas, the Legends Island
Circuit — a full R-class race around the island — and a second Horizon
Festival Site. The island is also the starting point for the biggest races
in the game, including the longest Goliath race yet.

=== How Do I Unlock Legend Island ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Campaign
How do I unlock Legend Island? Do the following:
1. Bank 32,500 points on the Horizon Festival progression path.
2. That unlocks the Horizon Legend wristband event, which starts from the
   same airfield as the Flight Club event that unlocked the Orange Expert
   Wristband.
3. Take the non-timed, non-competitive drive down to Legend Island, an
   island directly east of Tokyo Docks, joined by the cast of characters
   from Forza Horizon 6 and previous entries in the series. You are given a
   Lamborghini Aventador LP 780-4 Ultimate for the journey and you keep it
   once the race is over.
4. After the drive you receive the Gold Wristband: you are a Horizon Legend
   and Legend Island, its events and the huge endgame races are now open.

=== Legend Island Races ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Campaign
What races does the Gold Wristband unlock? The Gold Wristband opens a group
of enormous races echoing Forza Horizon 5's Goliath, one for each festival
discipline:
- The Goliath — a road racing loop around the perimeter of the entire map,
  starting from Legend Island, for R-class cars; long straights and
  snake-like mountain roads, and a ton of credits as the reward.
- The Colossus — a high-speed road race on the game's Highway Loop starting
  from Shimanoyama, limited to S2 cars, spent almost entirely above 200 mph.
- The Titan — the largest cross-country race in the game, starting in
  Ohtani and crossing rough terrain through many biomes, restricted to
  Total Buggies & Offroad cars.
- The Gauntlet — the biggest dirt race, starting in Takashiro and covering
  Sotoyama and Hokubu, restricted to Rally Monsters.
On Legend Island itself, the headline event is a R-class race around the
Legend Island Circuit.
