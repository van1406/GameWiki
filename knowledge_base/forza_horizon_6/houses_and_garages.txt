# Forza Horizon 6 — Player Houses, Garages and the Estate

Buying homes in Japan, customizing and sharing your garage, and building
freely at the Estate.

=== Player Houses ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Properties
How do player houses work? Like previous Horizon titles, properties around
Japan can be purchased to become your home — seven documented properties
across the country. Houses are unlocked progressively: collecting Stamps
for your Collection Journal unlocks new homes to buy, and at least one
house is placed in a documented location, with a player house for sale in
Ine Fishing Village near its boathouses. Buying a house gives you a base
with a customizable garage, and your stored cars double as fast travel
points across the map.

=== How Do I Buy a House ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Properties
How do I buy a player house? Progress your Discover Japan Stamps — Stamp
collection is what unlocks new homes to buy around Japan — then purchase
the property with credits when it becomes available. Example: the house in
Ine sits right by the village and a story mission sits nearby, rewarding
you for exploring that corner of the coast.

=== Customizable Garages ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Properties
How do I customize my garage? Every player house in Forza Horizon 6
features customizable garages — new to the series. In your garage you can:
- Choose which cars from your collection are displayed.
- Decorate the space freely to create the ideal showcase for your cars.
- Invite other players into your garage to see it.
- Download layouts shared online by the community, in the same way as
EventLab creations — if you do not want to decorate from scratch, grab a
community layout.
Your cars are stored across eight garages, and those garages also serve as
fast travel points, so a customized garage is both a showroom and a
handy anchor on the map.

=== The Estate ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Properties
What is the Estate? The Estate is a special property that unlocks after
you obtain your first Stamp, when Mei introduces you to her grandparent's
long-abandoned estate. It is a mountain valley where you can build freely
in the open world, creating nearly anything you can imagine:
- Build and decorate directly in the open world, not inside a menu-bound
  house.
- Each item costs credits to place down, but the cost is refunded if you
  delete it.
- Your creations are permanent and visitable by other players, so the
  Estate becomes a shareable, walkable showcase of your work.
It is described as creating "the home of your dreams" — the biggest
creative space in the game alongside EventLab.

=== Garages and Fast Travel ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Properties
How do garages and fast travel interact? You can store your unlocked
vehicles in eight garages, which also serve as fast travel points — so the
faster you collect cars and houses across Japan, the more of the map you
can jump between instantly. This makes buying houses and filling garages
part of practical progression, not just collecting.
