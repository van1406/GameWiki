# Forza Horizon 6 — Rewards, Credits, Car Pass and Editions

What you earn, how you earn credits and reward cars, and what each edition
and pass contains.

=== Rewards ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Rewards
What rewards are there in Forza Horizon 6? Documented rewards include:
- Credits, the currency used for cars, houses, upgrades and Estate items.
- Wheelspins and Super Wheelspins (rarer but more rewarding after the
  rebalance; available once you qualify for the festival).
- Cars — Season Rewards, Series Rewards, Championship rewards, Horizon Play
  rewards, loyalty cars and Gift Drops from other players.
- Car horns, QuickChat phrases and Forza LINK phrases (for example
  "QuickChat: Oooh… Shiny!" and "QuickChat: Beam me up!").
- Badges from Horizon Play ranks and challenge sets.
- Character items and cosmetics as playlist rewards.
- XP boards and player experience towards your level and prestige.

=== How Do I Earn Reward Cars ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Rewards
How do I earn reward cars? Documented routes to reward cars:
- Festival Playlist: Season Rewards at 15/30 points (Series 01 thresholds),
  Series Rewards at 60/120 points, each handing out exclusive cars.
- Championships: win the themed event and the listed car is yours (for
  example the Pagani Huayra R for the Modern Super Saloons championship).
- Horizon Play challenges: complete a mode challenge for a car (for
  example the 2022 Ferrari 296 GTB for playing Hide & Seek).
- Wristband progress and special events: the Porsche 911 Rallye arrives
  after the Orange Expert Wristband, and the Lamborghini Aventador LP 780-4
  Ultimate is given during the Horizon Legend event.
- Loyalty rewards: free cars for accounts that played previous Forza games.
- Festival milestones and community gifts: gift cars handed out to all
  players during certain windows.
- The Car Pass: 30 cars delivered one per week if you own it.

=== Earning Credits ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Rewards
How do I earn credits? Documented ways to make money:
- Finish races, Championships and events — big endgame races like The
  Goliath reward a ton of credits.
- Daily Challenges pay 5,000 credits each and Weekly Challenges 25,000 in
  Series 01; Treasure Hunts pay 100,000 credits.
- Seasonal jobs: the Raku-Raku food delivery job earns additional income,
  and completing a full Tokyo City Food Delivery shift is itself a
  challenge.
- Festival Playlist point thresholds pay credits alongside cars.
- Sell or trade cars through the Auction House.
- Stamps unlock Barn Find Rumors — cheap barn find restorations add rare
  cars to your collection instead of buying them at Autoshow prices.

=== Wheelspins ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Rewards
How do Wheelspins work? Wheelspins are the game's prize rolls, awarded for
progress and playlist activities (Time Attack, PR stunt and similar
rewards in the playlist are Wheelspins). Two documented changes in Forza
Horizon 6: you can only obtain Wheelspins once you have qualified for the
Horizon Festival, and they have been rebalanced to be rarer to earn
throughout the game and more rewarding when you do spin them, with Super
Wheelspins especially rare. Prizes include credits, cars and cosmetics.

=== Car Pass ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Rewards
What is the Car Pass? The Car Pass is downloadable content that adds 30
cars to your game, delivered one per week. It is included in the Deluxe and
Premium Editions of Forza Horizon 6 (and in the Premium Upgrade for Game
Pass holders). Because the cars arrive weekly, a Car Pass owner keeps
getting new cars spread across roughly 30 weeks rather than all at once.
Other documented content packs include the Welcome Pack, the Time Attack
Car Pack, the Italian Passion Car Pack and two premium expansions that
arrive post-launch.

=== Editions ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Rewards
What editions does Forza Horizon 6 have? Documented editions:
- Standard Edition ($69.99): the base game only — the tier offered with
  Xbox Game Pass Ultimate and PC Game Pass.
- Deluxe Edition ($99.99): base game plus the Car Pass and Welcome Pack.
- Premium Edition ($129.99): everything at launch — Car Pass, Welcome Pack,
  Time Attack Car Pack, Italian Passion Car Pack, VIP Membership and both
  premium expansions — plus four days of early access (from 15 May 2026).
Pre-ordering any edition grants an exclusive, pre-tuned Ferrari J50.
Online console multiplayer requires a Game Pass subscription that includes
online multiplayer, sold separately.

=== VIP Membership ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Rewards
What does VIP Membership include? VIP Membership is part of the Premium
Edition (and Premium Upgrade). Its documented in-game perk in this
knowledge base: exclusive VIP cars — the 2019 Toyota Tacoma TRD Pro Forza
Edition is only obtainable with the VIP Membership of the Premium Edition,
and the 1999 Dodge Viper GTS ACR Forza Edition is documented as available
via VIP Membership or the Premium Edition.

=== Loyalty Rewards ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Rewards
What loyalty reward cars can I get? If your Xbox Gamertag has played
previous Forza titles, Forza Horizon 6 gives you their cover cars for free,
placed in your garage after you complete the Tokyo City introductory race:
- Forza Horizon: 2013 Dodge SRT Viper GTS
- Forza Horizon 2: 2014 Lamborghini Huracan LP 610-4
- Forza Horizon 3: 2016 Lamborghini Centenario LP 770-4
- Forza Horizon 4: 2016 Aston Martin Vulcan (the McLaren Senna was not
  included at launch due to licensing)
- Forza Horizon 5: 2021 Mercedes-AMG ONE
- Forza Motorsport (2023): 2024 Chevrolet Corvette E-Ray
These arrive before you have earned much money, which makes loyalty
accounts start with a serious car collection.
