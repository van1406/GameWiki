# Forza Horizon 6 — Car Meets

Where the Car Meets are, what you can do at them, and how they fit into
Japanese car culture in the game.

=== Car Meets ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Multiplayer
How do Car Meets work? Car Meets are open-world social spaces returning
from Forza Horizon 3. At a Car Meet you can showcase your cars and your
character, download Tunes and Liveries from other players, download full
copies of other players' cars, explore the map together, and compete in
events with the people you meet. They are the game's nod to Japanese car
culture — urban street racing and Daikoku car meets were highlighted by the
developers as a core part of representing that culture — and Festival
Playlist challenges regularly ask you to park specific cars at a Car Meet
(for example "park the 1993 Autozam AZ-1 at any Car Meet" or "take a photo
of your car at the Festival Car Meet").

=== Car Meet Locations ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Multiplayer
Where are the Car Meets? There are three documented Car Meet locations in
Forza Horizon 6:
- The Horizon Festival site.
- The Okuibuki parking area, towards the alpine region ("the Alps" area).
- Daikoku, the famous Tokyo-area meet spot.
There are also evolving meetups referenced in festival content (the
"Evolving World Car Meet" appears in seasonal challenges), so meets can
rotate or update over time. Driving to a meet and parking up is all that is
required to join the session.

=== What Can I Do at a Car Meet ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Multiplayer
What can I do at a Car Meet? Documented activities:
- Show off your customized cars and your character to other players.
- Download Tunes, Liveries and even full copies of other players' cars
  directly at the meet.
- Take photos and complete photo challenges that target meets.
- Group up with people you meet and go driving or enter events together.
Car Meets are open-world and free-form — there is no fixed event structure,
they are the social hub side of the festival.

=== Drag Meets and Car Meets ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Multiplayer
What is the difference between a Car Meet and a Drag Meet? Car Meets are
social showcases for looking at builds and swapping tunes and liveries.
Drag Meets are competitive: open-world straight-line races for up to twelve
players with a synchronized start tree and in-game leaderboards, found at
locations such as the Urakawa Space Center. Both are open-world, both are
documented festival activities, and both show off Japanese car culture —
but a Drag Meet is where you prove your launch, while a Car Meet is where
you park and admire.
