# Forza Horizon 6 — Accessibility and Assistance Features

The accessibility options Forza Horizon 6 ships with, and the driving
assistance features that make the game approachable.

=== Accessibility ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Accessibility
What accessibility features does Forza Horizon 6 have? Forza Horizon 6 is
described as the most approachable Horizon game yet, and its documented
accessibility and assistance options include:
- Granular High Contrast Mode — a customizable high-contrast display mode.
- Car Proximity Radar — a proximity sensor that detects the cars around
  you.
- American and British Sign Language support (ASL and BSL).
- AutoDrive — select a destination and have the car driven there for you.
These sit alongside the usual controller and wheel support, ultrawide
monitor support, and Quality/Performance graphics modes so players can tune
the experience to their setup.

=== Accessibility Features ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Accessibility
What are the accessibility features in detail? Documented details:
- Granular High Contrast Mode: customizable high contrast so on-screen
  elements are easier to distinguish — "granular" means the mode can be
  adjusted rather than simply switched on.
- Car Proximity Radar: a proximity sensor that detects other cars around
  you, helping with spatial awareness in traffic and crowded streets.
- American and British Sign Language: sign language support is listed among
  the customization options that let everyone experience the cars, music
  and locations of the festival.
- AutoDrive: the car drives itself to a chosen destination (see the
  AutoDrive entry).
The official framing: "our customization options include Granular High
Contrast Mode, Car Proximity Radar, American and British Sign Language, and
AutoDrive, allowing everyone to experience the beautiful cars, hit music
and spectacular locations of the Horizon Festival."

=== AutoDrive ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Accessibility
How does AutoDrive work? AutoDrive is a driving assistance feature: you
select a destination, and the car is driven there for you. It reduces the
demand of manual driving for players who want to reach a location without
navigating the whole way — useful for long stretches of Japan's highways or
when you simply want to arrive somewhere and start an event. It is listed
as one of the four headline accessibility/approachability options in Forza
Horizon 6.

=== Car Proximity Radar ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Accessibility
How does the Car Proximity Radar work? The Car Proximity Radar is a
proximity sensor that detects the cars around you — a spatial-awareness aid
for dense traffic, Tokyo City streets and crowded meetup areas. It is one
of the documented customization options designed to make the game playable
by more people.

=== High Contrast Mode ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Accessibility
Is there a high contrast mode? Yes — Forza Horizon 6 includes a Granular
High Contrast Mode, documented as customizable rather than a single fixed
preset, so players can adjust contrast to suit their vision needs.

=== Performance and Display Options ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Accessibility
What display options are there? On Xbox consoles you can pick a Quality
mode (native 4K at 30 fps on Series X, 1440p at 30 fps on Series S) or a
Performance mode (targeting 60 fps with dynamic resolution on both). On PC
the game supports high uncapped framerates, 4K via DLSS 4, FSR 3+3 and
XeSS 2.1, ultrawide monitors, robust controller and wheel support, and
runs on Steam Deck and ROG Ally handhelds. These options let players favour
frame rate or fidelity depending on their display and hardware.
