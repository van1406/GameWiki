# Forza Horizon 6 — Progression and Wristbands

The three progression paths (Wristbands, Stamps and Horizon Play), how the
Wristband system and Horizon Qualifiers work, and how players level up.

=== Progression Paths ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Progression
Forza Horizon 6 has three main progression paths that run alongside each
other:
1. Wristbands — the campaign path through the Horizon Festival: complete
   races and special events to earn new Wristbands and access faster car
   classes.
2. Stamps — the Discover Japan path: everything you find, photograph and
   collect in Japan earns Stamps for your Collection Journal.
3. Horizon Play — the multiplayer path: play online game modes to earn XP,
   ranks and Badges.
These sit on top of the standard player levelling and prestige system that
represents your overall dedication to the game.

=== Wristbands ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Progression
Wristbands are your ticket to be invited to new events at the Horizon
Festival, and each new Wristband lets you take part in races with higher car
class restrictions. You earn them by completing races, events and stunts in
the campaign. There are 7 Festival Wristbands to earn in total, running from
the first Yellow Wristband up to the coveted Gold Wristband; among the tiers
documented here are Yellow, Green, Orange (the Expert Wristband) and Blue,
with the final tier being the Gold Wristband that marks you as a Horizon
Legend. Once you have accumulated enough progress, a new Wristband Event
becomes available — these are special events such as Showcase Events and new
Horizon Rush obstacle challenges, and you must complete them to obtain your
next Wristband.

=== How Wristbands Work ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Progression
How do Wristbands work? Your campaign progress is measured in Horizon
Festival Points. Racing in official festival events (Road, Dirt and Cross
Country Races, Time Attack Circuits, Drag Meets, PR Stunts and Bonus Boards)
earns you points; when you have accumulated enough, a new Wristband Event
unlocks. Complete that special event — a Showcase or a Horizon Rush
challenge — and you are awarded the next Wristband, which opens up invites
to events with faster car class restrictions. In total there are 7
Wristbands, and the 7th (Gold) unlocks Legend Island. To receive your very
first Wristband you must complete the Horizon Qualifiers and the Horizon
Invitational.

=== Horizon Qualifiers ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Progression
What are the Horizon Qualifiers? The Horizon Qualifiers are the entry events
you must complete as a tourist before the Horizon Festival will accept you.
Together with the Horizon Invitational, they are the two steps that earn you
your first Wristband and let you join the festival as a rookie driver. The
game's introduction takes you through these opening events — including a
race to the Urakawa Space Center — before the rest of the campaign opens up.

=== Horizon Invitational ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Progression
The Horizon Invitational is the final qualifier event that decides whether
you join the Horizon Festival. You must complete both the Horizon
Qualifiers and the Horizon Invitational to receive your first Wristband and
become a rookie festival driver.

=== Earning Horizon Festival Points ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Progression
How do I earn Festival Points? Horizon Festival Points come from everything
in the festival campaign: finishing Road, Dirt and Cross Country races,
Time Attack Circuits, Drag Meets, PR Stunts and Bonus Boards, plus the
special Wristband Events. Every Horizon Play level you reach (up to level
25) also grants Horizon Festival Points towards your next Wristband, and
other Horizon Play rewards can boost your campaign progression too. As a
reference point, reaching 32,500 points on the Horizon Festival progression
path unlocks the Horizon Legend wristband event that leads to Legend Island.

=== Stamps and the Collection Journal ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Progression
Everything you find in Japan earns progress towards your next Stamp. That
includes collecting and customising cars, photographing murals, smashing
mascots, finding landmarks, joining Mei on her day tours of Japan, completing
Street and Touge Races, and earning additional income at the Raku-Raku food
delivery job. Collecting Stamps for your Collection Journal unlocks Barn Find
Rumors and new homes to buy around Japan. There are 7 Stamps to obtain in
Discover Japan, inspired by Japan's long-standing tradition of recording
one's journey — a physical "proof of visit" that visualises your memories of
the trip. After you obtain your first Stamp, Mei introduces you to her
grandparent's long-abandoned Estate, where you can build in the open world.

=== Horizon Play Progression ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Progression
How does Horizon Play progression work? Horizon Play — the suite of online
game modes — has its own levelling system. You earn XP simply by playing
Horizon Play modes and obtain a new Badge for every 10 ranks you achieve, up
to Level 100. You also unlock extra Badges by completing a set number of
each type of Horizon Play mode and by earning Skills. Horizon Play has its
own Series Standing Leaderboards for each game mode, and they reset whenever
a new Festival Playlist Series begins. Progression crossover: each Horizon
Play level up to Level 25 grants Horizon Festival Points towards your next
Wristband.

=== Player Level and Prestige ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Progression
Alongside the three main paths, Forza Horizon 6 keeps the standard player
levelling and prestige system from previous Horizon games, which represents
your overall dedication to playing regardless of which activity you prefer.

=== Wheelspins ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Progression
When do I get Wheelspins? Wheelspins return in Forza Horizon 6, but you can
only obtain them once you have qualified for the Horizon Festival — that is,
after completing the Horizon Qualifiers and the Horizon Invitational. They
have been rebalanced to be rarer to earn throughout the game and more
rewarding when you do spin them, and Super Wheelspins are especially rare.
Wheelspins are also handed out as rewards in the Festival Playlist.
