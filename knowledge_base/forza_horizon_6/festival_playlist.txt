# Forza Horizon 6 — Festival Playlist

How the seasonal Festival Playlist works: points, Season Rewards, Series
Rewards and the challenge types inside it.

=== Festival Playlist ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Rewards
How does the Festival Playlist work? The Festival Playlist is Forza Horizon
6's seasonal schedule of events and challenges. Each season (which rotates
with the weekly game season) offers a fresh set of activities — weekly
challenges, daily challenges, photo challenges, treasure hunts,
championships, PR stunt targets, Time Attacks, Horizon Life events and
Horizon Play challenges — and completing them earns playlist points.
Accumulating points unlocks rewards at set thresholds: Season Rewards at
the low end (documented at 15 and 30 points in Series 01, rising to 20 and
40 in Series 02) and Series Rewards across the whole series (60 and 120
points in Series 01, 80 and 160 in Series 02). Some rewards are exclusive to
the Festival Playlist, so grab them while the season is active — they are
gone when the playlist rotates.

=== Types of Playlist Challenges ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Rewards
What challenge types are in the Festival Playlist? Documented types:
- Weekly Challenge — a themed multi-step challenge tied to one car (for
  example "Gazoo Racer: 2022 Toyota GR86; Speed Zones; Speed Skills;
  Touge"), worth 25,000 credits in Series 01.
- Daily Challenge — small tasks worth 5,000 credits each (win races, earn
  skills, take photos, honk a horn in a Honda, send a Gift Drop...).
- Photo Challenge — photograph a specific place or car (for example
  #TokyoTower at the base of Tokyo Tower, #FestivalVibes at the Horizon
  Festival Japan Site).
- Treasure Hunt — follow a photo clue to discover a treasure worth 100,000
  credits.
- Championship — a themed multi-race event with a car reward (for example
  "Street Fighter: Modern Super Saloons - A Class" rewarding the Pagani
  Huayra R).
- PR Stunt targets — Speed Traps, Speed Zones, Danger Signs and
  Trailblazers with class and score targets, rewarding Wheelspins.
- Time Attack and Drag Meet targets — set a time, reward a Wheelspin.
- Horizon Life events — The Trial and similar structured online events.
- Horizon Play challenges — play Eliminator, Hide & Seek, Touge Showdown,
  Spec Racing or Horizon Drift for car rewards.
- Seasonal Jobs — side-activity shifts such as Tokyo City Food Delivery.
- Collectibles — smash a set number of objects (for example 5 vending
  machines in Tokyo City).

=== Season Rewards and Series Rewards ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Rewards
How do Season and Series Rewards work? Season Rewards are the shorter-term
goals: reach the point threshold within the current season and the reward
car (or credits) is yours — documented Season 01 thresholds were 15 and 30
points, handing out cars like the 1999 Toyota Altezza RS200 Z Edition, 2006
Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution IX MR, 1997 Nissan Skyline GT-R V-Spec and a
1974 Toyota Corolla SR5. Series Rewards are the longer-term goals: bank
points across the whole series (60/120 points in Series 01) for bigger
prizes such as the 2008 Mazda Furai and 2010 Nissan 370Z. Each new Series
resets the playlist — for example Series 01 "Welcome To Japan" was followed
by Series 02 "Horizon Decades" — and the Series Standing Leaderboards reset
with it.

=== Series History Rewards ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Rewards
What are Series History Rewards? Beyond the current series, Forza Horizon 6
keeps long-term playlist goals alive: Series History Rewards include
exclusive cars unlocked based on your lifetime Festival Playlist
participation, so players who have engaged with the playlist over time
receive cars that newer players have not earned yet. Regular series also
carry their own reward structure — documented: Series 02 "Horizon Decades"
features 10 Series Reward cars to obtain and three Badges to add to your
collection, with rewards for every driver at the Horizon Festival.

=== Weekly and Daily Challenge Tips ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Rewards
How should I approach the playlist efficiently? Documented structure-based
advice: knock out the Weekly Challenge first — it is the biggest single
point source tied to one car you likely already own; clear Dailies as they
rotate (they are small but stack); grab Photo and Treasure Hunts since they
are quick; and remember that Horizon Play levels up to 25 and other online
rewards feed back into your Wristband progression, so playlist play
accelerates the campaign too. Exclusivity matters: rewards are only
available while their season playlist is active.
