# Forza Horizon 6 — Cars and the Car Collection

The 550+ car roster, Forza Edition and Aftermarket Cars, and how cars are
customised.

=== Cars ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Cars
Forza Horizon 6 launched with over 550 real-world cars, ranging from
high-powered sports cars to classic jeeps and minitrucks, and including
sports cars, off-roaders, hot hatchbacks, Kei cars, Time Attack cars and
much loved JDM classics. The roster covers decades of Japanese car culture
as well as the rest of the automotive world, and Playground Games promised
more than 550 cars at launch with additional cars arriving over time through
content updates and passes. You can inspect, collect, upgrade and customise
them, and they are stored across your garages.

=== Getting a New Car ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Cars
How do I get a new car? There are many documented ways to add cars to your
collection:
- Buy them from the Autoshow with credits.
- Bid on them at the Auction House.
- Win them by completing races, Championships and festival events.
- Earn them from the Festival Playlist as Season and Series Rewards.
- Spin Wheelspins and Super Wheelspins (available after you qualify for the
  festival).
- Find rare Forza Edition and Aftermarket Cars parked around the world and
  buy them at a discount.
- Receive them as loyalty rewards if your account played previous Forza
  games, or as gifts from other players.
- Receive new cars weekly through the Car Pass if you own it.

=== Forza Edition Cars ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Cars
What are Forza Edition cars? Forza Edition cars are special versions of cars
fitted with extreme modifications and unique liveries, making them stand out
from the standard car. They appear throughout Forza Horizon 6 as rewards —
for example the 1994 Mazda MX-5 Forza Edition from Festival Playlist
activities, the 2019 Toyota Tacoma TRD Pro Forza Edition from VIP Membership,
the 1999 Dodge Viper GTS ACR Forza Edition, and the 2012 Nissan GT-R Black
Edition R35 Forza Edition. Forza Horizon 6 also brought in crazier-than-ever
Forza Edition cars as part of its customisation improvements.

=== Aftermarket Cars ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Cars
What are Aftermarket Cars? Aftermarket Cars are rare special cars that can
be found parked around the world in set positions, waiting to be purchased
by the player at a discounted price compared to the Autoshow. You can test
drive them before buying. They are placed to reflect where you are and what
you are doing — for example, an Aftermarket Car can be found down the river
from Nachi Falls, and they are often spotted near Drag Strips and city-based
events. Completing a purchase of an Aftermarket Car even counts towards
festival challenges.

=== How Do I Get Aftermarket Cars ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Cars
How do I get Aftermarket Cars? Explore the open world and keep an eye out
for special cars parked at fixed spots — they are the Aftermarket Cars. They
show up in places that suit them, such as near drag strips, around city
events and next to landmarks like Nachi Falls. Walk up to one, test drive
it, and buy it for less than the Autoshow price. Some Forza Edition cars are
also found this way — for example the Mazda MX-5 Forza Edition appears as an
Aftermarket Car near drag strips and city events.

=== Car Customisation ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Cars
How do I customise my car? Forza Horizon 6 features many customisation
improvements: new body kits, new Forza-created parts and aerodynamics
(Forza Aero options), and the ability to paint custom liveries and stickers
on windows. You can fit different rims to the front and rear of your cars
independently, install engine swaps and drivetrain conversions, and paint
your car in liveries — either your own or ones shared by the community.
Cars are also more affected by the world: tire tread changes as you drive
more miles, engine audio has been remastered, and steering animations show
up to 540 degrees of wheel rotation.

=== Cover Cars ===
Game: Forza Horizon 6
Category: Cars
The Forza Horizon 6 cover art features the 2025 GR GT Prototype together
with a 2025 Toyota Land Cruiser 250, with Mount Fuji and Tokyo in the
background. The GR GT Prototype — a race-derived road car from Toyota Gazoo
Racing — is the game's cover car and its most modern featured machine.
