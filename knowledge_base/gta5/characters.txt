# GTA 5 — Characters

Profiles of the main playable characters and supporting cast of Grand Theft Auto V.

=== Michael De Santa ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Character
Role: Playable protagonist, former bank robber
Michael is a retired bank robber living in Rockford Hills with his wife Amanda and his
children Jimmy and Tracey. He lives under a secret deal with FIB agent Dave Norton,
who faked Michael's death during the North Yankton bank robbery. Michael struggles with
his family life and his urge to keep pulling heists. His special ability is Bullet Time,
which slows down time while aiming.
Favorite activities: Watching movies at the Eclipse Theater, drinking whiskey.

=== Trevor Philips ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Character
Role: Playable protagonist, career criminal
Trevor is a volatile career criminal and gunrunner who lives in Sandy Shores and runs
Trevor Philips Enterprises. He is Michael's former partner in crime and believes Michael
died in North Yankton until the story reunites them. Trevor's special ability is Red Mist,
which lets him take less damage and deal more damage while enraged.

=== Franklin Clinton ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Character
Role: Playable protagonist, aspiring gangster
Franklin is a young hustler from South Los Santos who leaves his old gang, the Families,
to work for richer clients. He is mentored by Michael and eventually becomes a partner
in heists. Franklin's special ability is Driving Focus, which slows time while driving
to let him steer through tight spots.

=== Lester Crest ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Character
Role: Heist planner and hacker
Lester is a brilliant strategist, hacker, and long-time partner of Michael and Trevor.
He plans every major heist in GTA 5, chooses the approach, and sells the crew's stolen
goods. He later becomes the head of SecuroServ and gives the protagonist assassination
missions that affect stock prices.

=== Devin Weston ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Character
Role: Antagonist, billionaire investor
Devin Weston is a ruthless billionaire venture capitalist who uses the crew for his own
gain and promises to make them rich. He is one of the main antagonists of the story.

=== Steve Haines ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Character
Role: Antagonist, FIB agent
Steve Haines is a corrupt FIB agent who blackmails Michael and Franklin into doing
dangerous jobs for the FIB against the IAA.

=== Lamar Davis ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Character
Role: Supporting character, gang member
Lamar is Franklin's loud-mouthed best friend and a member of the Grove Street Families.
He drags Franklin into trouble several times during the story.

=== Amanda De Santa ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Character
Role: Supporting character
Amanda is Michael's wife. She is obsessed with tennis and yoga and often clashes with
Michael over his lifestyle.

=== Tracey and Jimmy De Santa ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Character
Role: Supporting characters
Tracey is Michael's daughter who wants to be famous, and Jimmy is his lazy son who
spends his time playing video games.

=== Special Ability Mechanics ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
Each protagonist has a unique special ability that fills up by driving well, getting
headshots, or taking damage. Activating it slows time and turns the screen a
character-specific color: blue for Michael, green for Trevor, and yellow for Franklin.
Abilities can be used whenever the character-colored meter has charge — it empties
while the ability runs, then recharges when the ability is off. Activate with the
special-ability button (both thumbsticks pressed together on controllers).

=== Character Switching ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
How do I switch characters? Hold down on the D-pad to open the character switch
wheel, highlight Michael, Franklin, or Trevor, and release — the camera moves to
that character and you control them immediately. Each character is somewhere
different on the map: Michael in Rockford Hills, Franklin in South Los Santos,
Trevor in Sandy Shores. Switching also clears the wanted level of the character
you switched away from.

=== Who Is Michael ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Character
Michael De Santa (born Michael Townley) is a playable protagonist: a former bank
robber who faked his death in the North Yankton prologue and now lives in a Rockford
Hills mansion with Amanda, Jimmy, and Tracey. He plans the crew's heists with
Lester and his special ability is Bullet Time, which slows time while aiming.

=== Who Is Franklin ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Character
Franklin Clinton is a playable protagonist: a young hustler from South Los Santos
who leaves the Families gang to work with Michael and eventually co-plans the
heists. His special ability is Driving Focus, which slows time while driving to
steer through tight spots.

=== Who Is Trevor ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Character
Trevor Philips is a playable protagonist: a volatile career criminal and gunrunner
in Sandy Shores who runs Trevor Philips Enterprises and is Michael's old partner
in crime. His special ability is Red Mist, which lets him take less damage and
deal more damage while enraged.
