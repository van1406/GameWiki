# GTA 5 — Mission Guide

Structured walkthroughs of the main story missions in Grand Theft Auto V. Every mission
lists its overview, how to start, characters, location, objectives, step-by-step
completion, vehicles, weapons, events, completion, rewards, and tips.

=== Mission: Prologue ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Mission
Mission Overview:
- A bank robbery in Ludendorff, North Yankton, set 19 years before the main story.
  Michael and Trevor rob the bank while accomplices hold hostages, and the job ends in
  tragedy when Michael is shot by an unknown gunman who is later revealed to be Dave
  Norton.
How to Start:
- The Prologue is the first mission of the game and starts automatically when you
  begin a new game.
Characters:
- Michael, Trevor, the bank crew, hostages, police, and Dave Norton.
Location:
- Ludendorff, North Yankton — a snowy town that only appears in the prologue.
Objectives:
- Take control of the bank and hold the hostages.
- Get the money from the vault area.
- Fight past the police and reach the snowmobiles.
- Escape Ludendorff with the crew.
How to Complete:
1. Move through the bank with the crew and keep the hostages under control.
2. Grab the cash in the vault area.
3. When the police surround the building, fight your way out to the snowmobiles.
4. Flee across the snow and follow the crew to the escape point.
5. The mission ends with Michael being shot by Dave Norton, which fakes Michael's
   death and sets up the entire story.
Vehicles:
- Snowmobiles used for the escape.
Weapons:
- Firearms for the robbery and the escape shootout.
Mission Events:
- Hostage control, a police shootout, and the faked death of Michael.
Completion:
- The crew escapes North Yankton, and the story jumps ahead 19 years to Los Santos.
Rewards:
- Unlocks the main story of GTA 5.
Tips:
- The prologue doubles as the game's tutorial — stay behind cover during the shootout
  and follow the on-screen prompts.

=== Mission: The Jewel Store Job ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Mission
Mission Overview:
- The first proper heist in GTA 5. Lester Crest plans a robbery of the Vangelico
  jewelry store in Rockford Hills, and Michael must choose between a loud
  smash-and-grab and a quiet break-in.
How to Start:
- Complete the setup missions "Casing the Jewel Store" and "Vangelico Meet Up", buy
  the Carbine Rifles from Ammu-Nation if you want the Smart approach, then meet
  Lester at the garment factory planning room.
Characters:
- Michael (crew leader), Lester Crest (planner), hired gunman Karl Abolnik or
  Gustavo Mota, drivers, and a hacker chosen before the mission.
Location:
- Vangelico jewelry store, Portola Drive, Rockford Hills; the getaway ends at the
  crew lockup.
Objectives:
- Travel to the Vangelico jewelry store with the crew.
- Neutralize the guards or alarms, depending on the chosen approach.
- Take the jewels from the display cases.
- Escape the police response on the Loud approach (none on the Smart approach).
- Deliver the loot to the lockup.
How to Complete:
Loud approach:
1. Drive to the jewel store with Karl, Gustavo, and the crew.
2. Park the bike behind the store and throw a grenade into the skylight.
3. Enter the store, intimidate the civilians, and grab the jewels from the cases.
4. Fight the LSPD officers who arrive outside.
5. Ride the bikes into the sewer tunnel to escape the police.
6. Reach the lockup to finish the mission.
Smart approach (requires buying the Carbine Rifles from Ammu-Nation before the mission):
1. Meet the crew at the store with the rifles.
2. Use the tranquilizer darts on the guards on the roof of the building next door.
3. Drill through the roof and drop into the store.
4. Spray the gas canisters to knock out the civilians silently.
5. Take the jewels from the display cases without police involvement.
6. Leave the store calmly and deliver the loot to the lockup.
Vehicles:
- Motorcycles used for the Loud escape through the sewer; ordinary cars to reach the
  store on the Smart approach.
Weapons:
- Carbine Rifles for the Smart approach (bought from Ammu-Nation beforehand),
  grenades for the Loud approach skylight, and your equipped weapons for the
  police shootout.
Mission Events:
- A guard neutralization phase, the robbery itself, and on the Loud approach a full
  LSPD response with a bike chase into the sewers.
Completion:
- The crew delivers the jewels to the lockup, Lester fences the loot, and each
  crew member receives their cut. This completes the first heist and unlocks the
  rest of the heist strand.
Rewards:
- Loud approach: $4,946,150 total take (about $9,892,300 before cuts).
- Smart approach: $5,014,650 total take, with no wanted level during the heist.
- Lester's cut: 5%; gunman, driver, and buyer cuts are chosen by the player.
Tips:
- The Smart approach avoids the police entirely — buy the Carbine Rifles first.
- Higher-skilled crew members cost a larger cut but perform better.
- Gold medals reward objectives such as headshots and time limits on mission replay.

=== Mission: The Merryweather Heist ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Mission
Mission Overview:
- Trevor wants to steal a package from a Merryweather security convoy. The crew
  follows a convoy across Blaine County and then attacks a Merryweather ship in the
  ocean, choosing between a frontal assault or a submarine bomb.
How to Start:
- Given by Trevor Philips after completing "Blitz Play" and "Friends Reunited".
Characters:
- Trevor Philips, Michael, Franklin, hired crew, and Merryweather private soldiers.
Location:
- Convoy routes across Blaine County, and a Merryweather freighter in the ocean off
  the coast of Los Santos.
Objectives:
- Follow the Merryweather convoy through Blaine County and learn where the package
  is taken.
- Get out to the Merryweather ship at sea.
- Steal the package from the ship.
- Escape back to shore with the package.
How to Complete:
1. Follow the convoy by road without losing it and watch it deliver the package to
   the freighter.
2. Choose an approach for the ship attack:
   - Frontal assault: take a boat out with the crew, fight the Merryweather guards
     on deck, grab the package, and return to shore.
   - Submarine: pilot the submarine under the hull, plant a bomb, and retrieve the
     package after the blast.
3. Fight off Merryweather soldiers during the escape.
4. Deliver the package to finish the mission.
Vehicles:
- Road vehicles for the convoy tail, a boat for the assault approach, and a
  submarine for the bomb approach.
Weapons:
- Your equipped weapons; Merryweather guards are well-armed private military
  contractors, so bring rifles and explosives.
Mission Events:
- A cross-county convoy tail, a naval battle, and the first direct clash between the
  crew and Merryweather.
Completion:
- The crew escapes with the package and the story advances; Merryweather remains an
  enemy for the rest of the game.
Rewards:
- No direct cash payout, but the mission advances the story.
Tips:
- Keep a safe distance while following the convoy — getting too close or too far
  fails the tail.
- The submarine approach causes less direct firefighting; the assault approach is a
  straight shootout.

=== Mission: Blitz Play ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Mission
Mission Overview:
- A setup mission for the Union Depository approach. The crew steals an armored
  Granger by ramming it off the road, tows it to a lockup, and repaints it so it can
  be used later.
How to Start:
- Given by Lester Crest and Martin Madrazo during the heist strand.
Characters:
- Michael, Franklin, Trevor, Lester, and hired crew members.
Location:
- Fleeca Bank and an armored truck depot in Los Santos, ending at a lockup.
Objectives:
- Intercept and ram the armored vehicle off the road.
- Tow the damaged vehicle back to the lockup.
- Repaint and repair the vehicle for future use.
How to Complete:
1. Take the tow truck with the crew and find the armored Granger on the road.
2. Ram or hook the vehicle and drag it off the road.
3. Tow it back to the lockup.
4. Repaint and repair the vehicle so it is ready for the heist preparations.
Vehicles:
- A tow truck and the stolen armored Granger.
Weapons:
- Standard weapons for defense during the intercept.
Mission Events:
- A road intercept and vehicle recovery that sets up the final heist.
Completion:
- The armored vehicle is secured, unlocking further heist setups.
Rewards:
- Unlocks further heist setups.
Tips:
- Ram the target vehicle against a solid obstacle so it stops quickly and the tow is
  easier.

=== Mission: The Paleto Score ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Mission
Mission Overview:
- The crew robs the Blaine County Savings Bank in Paleto Bay using an armored truck
  disguised as an FIB vehicle. The plan fails when the truck breaks down and the crew
  must fight through police and military forces on the main street before escaping by
  train.
How to Start:
- Given by Lester Crest after completing "The Merryweather Heist" and "Blitz Play".
Characters:
- Michael, Trevor, Franklin, Lester, and hired crew.
Location:
- Blaine County Savings Bank, Paleto Bay; the escape runs down the main street and
  onto the railway line.
Objectives:
- Drive the disguised armored truck to the bank in Paleto Bay.
- Blow the vault and take the money.
- Survive the police and military response on Paleto Bay's main street.
- Reach the train and escape.
How to Complete:
1. Arrive at the bank in the disguised truck.
2. Blast or drill into the vault and load the cash.
3. The getaway vehicle breaks down outside — fight through the police and National
   Guard forces along the main street.
4. Regroup and escape on the train.
5. Reach safety to finish the mission.
Vehicles:
- The armored truck disguised as an FIB vehicle, and the train used for the escape.
Weapons:
- Heavy weapons are recommended: the fight on the main street includes police and
  military resistance, so rifles, snipers, and explosives help.
Mission Events:
- The vault robbery, a vehicle breakdown, a full-scale street battle, and a train
  escape.
Completion:
- The crew gets out of Paleto Bay with the money, moving the heist strand toward the
  final score.
Rewards:
- About $8,160,000 total take, split between the crew.
Tips:
- Stock up on armor, snacks, and explosives before starting — the street fight is one
  of the longest firefights in the game.

=== Mission: The Big Score ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Mission
Mission Overview:
- The final and largest heist of GTA 5: stealing gold from the Union Depository in
  downtown Los Santos.
How to Start:
- Given by Lester Crest after completing "The Paleto Score" and "Architect's Plans".
Characters:
- Michael, Franklin, Trevor, Lester, and the hired gunman, driver, and hacker.
Location:
- Union Depository, Downtown Los Santos, with escapes through the subway tunnels or
  across downtown.
Objectives:
- Get into the Union Depository and reach the gold vault.
- Load out the gold.
- Escape Los Santos with the gold and deliver it to the buyer.
How to Complete:
Subtle approach:
1. The crew infiltrates the depository using a drill and a garbage truck.
2. Load the gold and walk out disguised as armored guards.
3. Escape through the subway tunnels.
4. Deliver the gold to the buyer to complete the heist.
Obvious approach:
1. The crew uses a tow cable to drag a vault door off a truck.
2. Fight across downtown Los Santos in a four-man crew and repel the police with
   firepower.
3. Fly the gold out of the city.
Vehicles:
- A drill, a garbage truck, and getaway cars for the subtle approach; helicopters
  and armored vehicles for the obvious approach.
Weapons:
- The obvious approach is a running gun battle — bring heavy weapons, body armor,
  and explosives.
Mission Events:
- Vault infiltration, an escape through the city, and a large police response on the
  obvious route.
Completion:
- The gold is delivered, Lester takes his cut, and each protagonist receives their
  share, leading into the final mission.
Rewards:
- Up to $1,200,000,000 total take, with the largest share depending on crew cuts.
Tips:
- Choose high-skill crew for gunman and driver roles — their cuts are worth it on the
  biggest payout in the game.
- The subtle approach avoids most of the fight; the obvious approach is faster but
  heavily contested.

=== Mission: Ending Choices ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Mission
Mission Overview:
- The final mission offers three endings. The canon ending is "Ending C — Deathwish",
  in which Michael, Trevor, and Franklin work together to kill Devin Weston, Steve
  Haines, and Stretch. The other two options let Franklin kill either Trevor or
  Michael.
How to Start:
- Reach the final mission after The Big Score; Devin Weston orders Franklin to kill
  Michael, and Steve Haines orders him to kill Trevor.
Characters:
- Franklin Clinton, Michael De Santa, Trevor Philips, Devin Weston, Steve Haines,
  Stretch, and Lester Crest (phone guidance).
Location:
- Los Santos — the three targets are dealt with in different parts of the city and
  Blaine County.
Objectives:
- Choose an ending: kill Trevor, kill Michael, or the Deathwish (Ending C) plan.
- On Ending C, eliminate all three targets with the crew working together.
How to Complete (Ending C — Deathwish):
1. Call Lester for a plan to save everyone.
2. Split the three targets between Franklin, Michael, and Trevor.
3. Each protagonist eliminates their target with the crew's support.
4. Survive the final confrontations and meet at the Vinewood sign.
5. The three friends reconcile and the story ends.
Vehicles:
- Personal vehicles for moving between targets; a helicopter appears in parts of the
  final mission.
Weapons:
- Bring your best weapons — the ending involves multiple firefights.
Mission Events:
- The choice itself, three coordinated hits, and the final reconciliation.
Completion:
- Ending C completes the game with all three protagonists alive; the epilogue shows
  their lives after the final job.
Rewards:
- Completing the story unlocks the full map for free roam with all three characters.
Tips:
- Ending C is the canonical ending — the other two choices permanently kill a
  protagonist, so save before deciding.

=== General Mission Mechanics ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
- Gold medals require optional objectives such as headshots, time limits, or
  specific vehicles used.
- Failed missions can be restarted instantly from the pause menu.
- Crew members take a percentage of every heist take; higher-skilled crew members
  cost more but can earn more money overall.
- Some missions require buying equipment beforehand, such as rifles for the
  Smart approach of The Jewel Store Job.
- Mission objectives are listed in the top right of the screen and map markers show
  where to go next.
