# GTA 5 — Wanted Level

How the wanted level system works in Grand Theft Auto V and exactly how to remove
wanted levels from 1 to 5 stars.

=== Wanted Level ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
The wanted level is the star rating shown in the top-right corner of the screen,
from 1 to 5 stars. Crimes committed in front of witnesses raise it: punching
civilians, hitting police, stealing cars with witnesses present, and shooting
people all add stars. Police respond with more force for every star — cars,
roadblocks, helicopters, and tactical teams. The wanted level stays active until
you escape: break line of sight with every officer and stay hidden until the
escape meter empties and the stars flash away. Committing more crimes while wanted
keeps the meter topped up.

=== How the Wanted Level System Works ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
- Each star adds a heavier police response: patrol officers, cruisers, roadblocks,
  police helicopters, and NOOSE tactical teams.
- While any officer can see you, the escape meter does not drop.
- Once you are out of sight, the circular escape meter under the stars drains; when
  it empties, the wanted level is removed.
- Getting spotted again stops the drain, so stay hidden until the meter is gone.
- Dying or being busted also clears the wanted level, but costs hospital or bail
  money.
- Switching to another protagonist clears the wanted level of the character you
  switched away from.

=== Removing a 1-Star Wanted Level ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
How do I lose a 1-star wanted level? A single star means a single patrol response.
Get out of the officers' line of sight — run around a corner, into an alley, or just
keep driving until the blue cones on the minimap lose you — then stop or stay in
cover while the small escape meter drains. A 1-star level is normally cleared in a
few seconds of breaking line of sight; you rarely need to hide for long.

=== Removing a 2-Star Wanted Level ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
How do I lose a 2-star wanted level? Two stars bring more cruisers that will chase
and ram you. Break line of sight first by taking fast turns out of the police cones
on the minimap, then get out of view — duck into an alley, a side street, or a
parking structure — and wait for the escape meter to drain. If cruisers are still
following, keep moving until none of them can see you, then hide on foot or in a
parked car around a corner.

=== Removing a 3-Star Wanted Level ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
How do I lose a 3-star wanted level? Three stars add a police helicopter that
tracks you from above, so staying on open streets will not work. Break the ground
units' line of sight and then get under cover from the air: drive through alleys,
under overpasses, into tunnels, or into parking garages so the helicopter cannot
see you. Stay hidden until the escape meter drains. Fighting every cruiser is a
losing battle — evasion is the reliable method at 3 stars.

=== Removing a 4-Star Wanted Level ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
How do I lose a 4-star wanted level? Four stars bring NOOSE tactical teams and more
aggressive roadblocks on top of the cruisers and helicopter. Do not try to shoot it
out: get a fast vehicle, break line of sight by turning off main roads, and use
tunnels, underpasses, and covered parking to hide from the helicopter. Leave the
vehicle somewhere out of sight, hide on foot around a corner or behind a building,
and wait for the escape meter to finish draining.

=== Removing a 5-Star Wanted Level ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
How do I remove, lose, get rid of, or escape a 5-star wanted level? Five stars is
the maximum police response: NOOSE teams, helicopters, and constant roadblocks
everywhere. Fighting is almost never worth it. The reliable way to clear 5 stars is
to break line of sight and hide until the escape meter empties:
1. Get out of the open — drive fast off the main roads into alleys, tunnels,
   underpasses, parking garages, or covered areas the helicopter cannot see into.
2. Keep moving until the police cones on the minimap lose you.
3. Leave your vehicle somewhere out of sight and hide on foot behind a wall or in a
   corner.
4. Stay hidden while the escape meter drains — if a cop spots you, the drain stops,
   so relocate deeper into cover.
5. When the meter empties the stars flash and disappear, and the wanted level is
   removed.
Switching characters or fast-traveling after death also clears it, at the cost of
hospital bills.

=== How to Evade the Police ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
How do I evade police in GTA 5? Evasion has two parts: breaking line of sight and
staying hidden. Drive or run out of the blue vision cones shown on the minimap —
sharp turns, alleys, and tunnels break pursuit quickly. Once no officer can see you,
the escape meter starts draining; stay out of sight until it finishes. Helicopters
at 3 stars and above keep visual contact from the air, so move under cover such as
overpasses, tunnels, and parking structures. Roadblocks at high stars are best
rammed or avoided by turning off the road before reaching them.

=== Hiding from the Police ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
How does hiding from police work? Hiding means staying out of the sight of every
police unit, including the helicopter. Good hiding spots are alleys behind
buildings, tunnels and underpasses, parking garages, and open lots away from street
lights and patrols. The escape meter under the stars only drains while you are not
seen; being spotted pauses it, so move deeper into cover instead of staying put if
officers are circling. Staying inside a personal vehicle is fine as long as nothing
can see it — but leaving the car somewhere obvious makes it easier for patrols to
spot you.

=== Crimes and How Stars Are Added ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
Minor crimes in front of witnesses (assault, car theft, hitting civilians) start at
1 star. Shooting at police, blowing up vehicles, and attacking people in view of
officers quickly raise the rating toward 3 stars, and serious violence or attacking
NOOSE units pushes it to 4 or 5. Crimes committed with no witnesses around do not
add stars at all, so isolated locations are safer. Killing witnesses before they
call the police can stop a wanted level from starting in the first place.
