# GTA 5 — Money, Businesses, and Properties

How money works in GTA 5: income sources, purchasable properties, businesses, and
the stock market.

=== Making Money in GTA 5 ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
How do characters earn money in GTA 5? The main source is heists — each one pays
millions, with the Big Score paying the most. On top of that: story and stranger
missions pay cash, robbing stores (aim a weapon at the clerk until they hand over
the register) gives quick money but may raise a wanted level, completing stunt
jumps and races pays rewards, and properties and businesses generate income
passively over time. Every protagonist starts with their own share of past heist
takings.

=== Properties ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
How do properties work? Properties are bought from map locations marked with a
dollar sign — garages, safehouses, a marina, an airfield, a taxi company, a cinema,
and more. Each purchased property generates income over time, shown in its
description, and some unlock extra side missions or perks (for example taxi
missions or movie theater revenue). Buying property is a long-term investment:
cash accumulates as you play, and property missions tied to the purchase can pay
back the cost quickly.

=== Businesses ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
How do businesses work? Owned businesses such as the Downtown Cab Co., the Vinewood
cinema, the marina, and the airfield work as income-generating assets: they pay
out over time automatically after purchase. Some businesses come with side missions
that raise their earnings. Businesses are bought the same way as properties — find
the for-sale blip on the map, purchase it, and the passive income begins.

=== Stock Market ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
Can I invest money in GTA 5? Yes — two stock markets exist: LCN (affected by
in-game events) and BAWSAQ (tied to player activity). Lester's assassination
missions are designed around the stock market: invest in the target company's
competitor before the mission and the stock swings afterward for a profit. The
stock market is accessed through the in-game phone or computer browser under
Financial Services.

=== Robbing Stores ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
How do I rob a store? Walk into any shop, aim a weapon at the clerk, and wait for
them to hand over the cash from the register. Leaving before they finish, or
shooting, can raise a wanted level. Store robberies are quick one-off payouts and
the clerk's fear meter fills faster if you keep the gun aimed.

=== Spending Money ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
Where does money go? Cash is spent on vehicles and property from websites,
upgrades at Los Santos Customs and Ammu-Nation, weapons and ammunition, clothes,
haircuts, and bribes such as hospital and bail fees when you are busted or killed.
Keeping a stock of armor and ammo before missions is worth the cost — hospital
bills from failed fights cost more than the armor.
