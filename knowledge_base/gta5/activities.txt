# GTA 5 — Side Activities

What activities are available in GTA 5, how they work, and where they are found.

=== Strangers and Freaks ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Side Activity
What are Strangers and Freaks? Side missions given by people met around the map,
marked with colored letter icons (a yellow letter for the contact). They range from
race challenges and missions for contacts like Tonya, Dom, and Barry to random
encounters that trigger when you drive past a glowing character. They pay cash and
often unlock vehicles or new activity strands.

=== Random Encounters ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Side Activity
Random encounters are one-off events that trigger at specific map spots — a
mugging, a crashed vehicle, a person needing a ride. They appear as question-mark
or event blips as you explore, reward cash or items, and some repeat until dealt
with.

=== Street Races ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Side Activity
How do races work? Race strands are started by contacts (like Simeon or SOS
Friends) and are marked on the map with checkered flags — street races, off-road
races, and sea races exist. Win to earn cash and unlock faster cars; Franklin's
Driving Focus special ability helps in tight corners.

=== Stunt Jumps ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Side Activity
Where are stunt jumps? Ramp cars and wrecks marked with a crown icon on the map
launch you over gaps, into billboards, or through flaming rings. Hitting the jump
correctly (centered and at speed) completes it, pays cash, and counts toward 100%
completion. Missing the landing just wastes the attempt — you can retry immediately.

=== Golf ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Side Activity
Where can I play golf? The golf club in Rockford Hills (Richman Glen) has a full
18-hole course. Walk up to the clubhouse to start a round; the mini-game uses a
power-and-accuracy swing meter. Winning pays cash and it works as a relaxing
break between missions.

=== Tennis ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Side Activity
Where can I play tennis? Public tennis courts around Los Santos (including one
near Vespucci) can be used by walking onto the court. The game plays as a simple
rally-and-serve mini-game; Amanda, Michael's wife, is also a tennis fan in the
story.

=== Shooting Range ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Side Activity
Where is the shooting range? Ammu-Nation stores with a range (marked on the map)
host shooting challenges with pistols, SMGs, and shotguns. Score points by hitting
targets within the time limit to win cash and improve your aim rating.

=== Flight School ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Side Activity
Where is flight school? The flight school at Los Santos International Airport
teaches flying with challenges in planes, helicopters, and jets. Completing lessons
unlocks aircraft for purchase and improves flying skill, which reduces turbulence
and improves handling.

=== Yoga and Other Diversions ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Side Activity
Michael can do yoga at his house, and other diversions include scuba diving at
wreck sites, base jumping from high points with a parachute, and visiting the
cinema. Each activity raises related skills or simply provides flavor around the
map.

=== Side Activity Completion ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Side Activity
How do I find activities? Open the pause-menu map and filter or look for blips:
races (checkered flags), stunt jumps (crowns), strangers (letters), shooting
ranges (guns), and sports courts. Completing activities counts toward 100% game
completion and pays cash rewards.
