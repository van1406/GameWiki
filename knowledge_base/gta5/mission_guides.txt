# GTA 5 — Mission Walkthroughs

Practical step-by-step answers for questions like "How do I complete this mission?",
"How do I finish this mission?", "What are the objectives?", and "Where do I go?".

=== Walkthrough: The Jewel Store Job ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Mission Walkthrough
Question this answers: How do I complete or finish the Jewel Store Job heist, and
what are its objectives?
Objectives in order:
1. Get to the Vangelico jewelry store on Portola Drive, Rockford Hills with the crew.
2. Deal with the guards and alarms using the approach you chose.
3. Take all the jewels from the display cases.
4. Escape — fight the police on the Loud approach, or walk out clean on the Smart
   approach.
5. Deliver the loot to the lockup to complete the mission.
Where to go:
- The store is in Rockford Hills; the planning room where the mission starts is
  Lester's garment factory downtown; the mission ends at the crew lockup.
How to finish it:
- Loud: grenade through the skylight, grab the jewels, fight the LSPD outside, then
  ride the bikes into the sewer tunnel and reach the lockup.
- Smart: tranquilize the roof guards, drill through the roof, gas the civilians,
  take the jewels quietly, and leave without a wanted level.
Tips:
- Buy the Carbine Rifles from Ammu-Nation before starting if you plan the Smart
  approach, or the mission forces the Loud route.

=== Walkthrough: The Merryweather Heist ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Mission Walkthrough
Question this answers: How do I complete the Merryweather Heist and what are the
objectives?
Objectives in order:
1. Follow the Merryweather convoy across Blaine County without losing it.
2. Track the package to the freighter at sea.
3. Attack the ship using one of two approaches.
4. Grab the package and escape back to shore.
Where to go:
- The convoy drives through Blaine County roads; the ship is off the coast in the
  Pacific Ocean.
How to finish it:
- Assault approach: take a boat with the crew, clear the guards on the deck, take
  the package, and sail back.
- Submarine approach: pilot the submarine under the hull, plant the bomb, then
  retrieve the package.
Tips:
- Keep your distance during the tail — too close or too far and the mission fails.

=== Walkthrough: Blitz Play ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Mission Walkthrough
Question this answers: How do I complete Blitz Play and what are the objectives?
Objectives in order:
1. Get the tow truck with the crew.
2. Find the armored Granger on the road and ram it off the road.
3. Tow the vehicle back to the lockup.
4. Repaint and repair it for later heist setups.
Where to go:
- The intercept happens on the Los Santos roads; the lockup is where the vehicle is
  stored and rebuilt.
How to finish it:
- Hook the armored vehicle with the tow truck, drag it to the lockup, and finish the
  repaint cutscene.
Tips:
- Ram the target into a wall or barrier so it stops quickly.

=== Walkthrough: The Paleto Score ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Mission Walkthrough
Question this answers: How do I complete the Paleto Score heist and what are the
objectives?
Objectives in order:
1. Drive the FIB-disguised armored truck to the Blaine County Savings Bank in
   Paleto Bay.
2. Open the vault and take the money.
3. Fight through the police and military response on the main street after the
   truck breaks down.
4. Reach the railway and escape by train.
Where to go:
- Paleto Bay on the north coast; the bank sits on the main street, and the train
  line runs behind the town.
How to finish it:
- Blast the vault, load the cash, survive the street battle with heavy weapons, and
  board the train to escape.
Tips:
- Buy armor, snacks, and explosives first — this is one of the game's biggest
  firefights.

=== Walkthrough: The Big Score ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Mission Walkthrough
Question this answers: How do I complete the Big Score, the final GTA 5 heist, and
what are the objectives?
Objectives in order:
1. Get into the Union Depository in downtown Los Santos.
2. Reach the gold vault and load out the gold.
3. Escape the city and deliver the gold to the buyer.
Where to go:
- The Union Depository is in downtown Los Santos; escapes run through the subway
  tunnels (subtle) or across downtown to the helicopters (obvious).
How to finish it:
- Subtle: drill in with the garbage truck, walk out as armored guards, escape
  through the tunnels.
- Obvious: tow the vault door off with a cable, fight across downtown, and fly the
  gold out.
Tips:
- Crew cuts matter most here — hire skilled gunman, driver, and hacker for the
  biggest payout.

=== Walkthrough: Prologue ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Mission Walkthrough
Question this answers: How do I complete the Prologue and what are the objectives?
Objectives in order:
- Rob the bank in Ludendorff and control the hostages.
- Get the cash from the vault.
- Fight the police to the snowmobiles.
- Escape North Yankton.
How to finish it:
- Follow the crew through the bank, take the money, fight out to the snowmobiles,
  and flee across the snow to the escape point — the mission ends with Michael being
  shot by Dave Norton.
Tips:
- This is the tutorial mission: stay in cover and follow the prompts.

=== Walkthrough: Ending Choices ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Mission Walkthrough
Question this answers: How do I finish the final mission of GTA 5 and what happens?
How to finish it:
- The canon route is Ending C (Deathwish): Lester helps plan one final job where
  Franklin, Michael, and Trevor work together to eliminate Stretch, Steve Haines,
  and Devin Weston, then meet at the Vinewood sign.
Where to go:
- Each protagonist goes after their own target across Los Santos and Blaine County.
Tips:
- Save your game before choosing — the other two endings permanently kill Michael or
  Trevor.
