# GTA 5 — Vehicles

Overview of notable vehicles in Grand Theft Auto V by category.

=== Sports and Super Cars ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Vehicles
Infernus: A classic supercar with very high top speed, inspired by the Lamborghini Diablo.
Zentorno: Mid-engine supercar with sharp styling and excellent acceleration, a fan favorite.
Truffade Adder: Hypercar based on the Bugatti Veyron; one of the fastest cars in the game.
Comet: Rear-engine sports car based on the Porsche 911.
Elegy RH8: Free high-performance sports car available through the Legendary Motorsport
website after linking a Rockstar account.

=== Muscle Cars ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Vehicles
Dominator: Aggressive muscle car with strong acceleration but loose handling.
Gauntlet: Classic American muscle car with balanced performance.
Sabre Turbo: Vintage muscle car often used by gangs in South Los Santos.

=== Motorcycles ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Vehicles
Bati 801: Fast sports bike, ideal for quick getaways and races.
Sanchez: Off-road dirt bike used in countryside missions.
Faggio: Slow but iconic scooter found all over Los Santos.
Blazer: All-terrain quad bike; used in some Trevor's missions in Blaine County.

=== Emergency and Utility ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Vehicles
Police Cruiser: Standard LSPD patrol car with decent speed.
Police Riot Van: Armored van used during wanted levels.
Fire Truck: Emergency vehicle with a water cannon.
Ambulance: Heals the player when entering with low health after a job.

=== Aircraft ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Vehicles
Buzzard Attack Chopper: Armed helicopter with homing missiles; purchase from
Warstock Cache & Carry for about $1,750,000.
Maverick: Civilian helicopter used for sightseeing and escaping.
P-996 LAZER: Military fighter jet found at Fort Zancudo; highly wanted if stolen.
Miljet: Large transport plane.
Dodo: Seaplane that can land on water.

=== Boats ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Vehicles
Dinghy: Small rubber boat used in coastal missions.
Jetsam: Luxury yacht available for purchase through the Dynasty 8 website.
Seashark: Fast jet ski found at beaches and marinas.

=== Off-Road and SUVs ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Vehicles
Karin Kuruma (Armored): Armored sports sedan with bulletproof windows, highly
recommended for drive-by missions and heist setups; bought from Southern San Andreas
Super Autos.
Granger: Full-size SUV frequently used by gangs and as an FIB vehicle.
Baller: Luxury SUV based on the Range Rover.

=== Finding, Buying, and Storing Vehicles ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
How do I find or get a vehicle? Steal parked cars from the street, call the
mechanic to deliver your personal vehicle, or buy cars from the in-game websites:
Legendary Motorsport (sports and super cars), Southern San Andreas Super Autos
(everyday cars), and Warstock Cache & Carry (military vehicles like the Buzzard).
Unsold vehicles spawn in their natural areas — supercars around Rockford Hills,
off-road trucks in Blaine County, aircraft at Los Santos International Airport and
Sandy Shores Airfield. Store a car by driving it into a safehouse garage; it
becomes a personal vehicle that insurance lets the mechanic replace if destroyed.
Customize any car at Los Santos Customs with engine, brakes, turbo, armor, paint,
and more.

=== Vehicle Mechanics ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
- Vehicles can be upgraded at Los Santos Customs with new engines, brakes, turbo,
  transmission, armor, and paint jobs.
- Every vehicle has visible damage states: the engine smokes and eventually fails if
  it takes too much damage.
- The player can call the mechanic to deliver a personal vehicle, and insurance makes
  stolen personal cars replaceable after destruction.
- Vehicles do not use fuel; they run indefinitely as long as the engine is not destroyed.
