# GTA 5 — Heists

The heist system in GTA 5: what heists are, how to prepare them, approaches, crew
choices, and payouts.

=== What Is a Heist in GTA 5 ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
A heist is a large planned robbery that Lester Crest organizes for the crew. Every
heist has preparation missions, crew roles (gunmen, drivers, and hackers), and often
two approaches — a loud one and a quiet one. Heists are the biggest source of money
in the game and are started after their setup missions are complete.

=== Heist: The Jewel Store Job ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Heist
What it is: The first heist of the game — a robbery of the Vangelico jewelry store
in Rockford Hills.
Preparations: Complete "Casing the Jewel Store" and "Vangelico Meet Up". For the
Smart approach, buy the Carbine Rifles from Ammu-Nation before the mission.
Approaches: Loud (grenade through the skylight, grab jewels, fight police out) or
Smart (tranquilize roof guards, drill through the roof, gas the civilians, walk out
clean).
Participants: Michael, Lester as planner, plus a hired gunman, driver, and hacker.
Objectives: Reach the store, defeat the guards or alarms, take the jewels, escape,
deliver the loot to the lockup.
Payout: Loud about $4,946,150 total take; Smart about $5,014,650 with no wanted
level. Lester takes 5%.

=== Heist: The Merryweather Heist ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Heist
What it is: Stealing a package from Merryweather security by attacking their ship
at sea.
Preparations: Complete "Blitz Play" and "Friends Reunited".
Approaches: Assault the freighter with a boat and crew, or use a submarine to plant
a bomb under the hull.
Participants: Trevor as planner, Michael, Franklin, and hired crew.
Objectives: Tail the convoy in Blaine County, hit the ship, take the package,
escape to shore.
Payout: No direct cash — the mission advances the main story.

=== Heist: The Paleto Score ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Heist
What it is: A bank robbery in Paleto Bay using an armored truck disguised as an FIB
vehicle.
Preparations: Complete "The Merryweather Heist" and "Blitz Play".
Approaches: One route — the truck breaks down and the crew fights out of town.
Participants: Michael, Trevor, Franklin, Lester, and hired crew.
Objectives: Drive to the bank, take the money, survive the police and military
response, escape by train.
Payout: About $8,160,000 total take, split between the crew.

=== Heist: The Big Score ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Heist
What it is: The final and largest heist — stealing gold from the Union Depository.
Preparations: Complete "The Paleto Score" and "Architect's Plans".
Approaches: Subtle (drill, garbage truck, walk out as guards, subway escape) or
Obvious (tow cable on the vault door, fight across downtown, fly the gold out).
Participants: Michael, Franklin, Trevor, Lester, plus gunman, driver, and hacker.
Objectives: Enter the depository, load the gold, escape Los Santos, deliver the
gold to the buyer.
Payout: Up to $1,200,000,000 total take — the biggest payout in the game.

=== Choosing Heist Approaches ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
Lester presents two approaches before each major heist. The loud approach is a
direct fight with bigger risks and police response; the quiet approach requires
extra equipment or preparation but avoids much of the combat. The chosen approach
changes the objectives and sometimes the payout — the Smart approach of the Jewel
Store Job pays slightly more and skips the wanted level entirely.

=== Choosing Crew and Cuts ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
Before each heist you pick gunman, drivers, and a hacker, and set each member's
percentage cut. Skilled crew cost more but do their jobs better: better gunmen
provide more covering fire, better drivers make faster getaways, and better hackers
shorten hacking sequences and raise the take. Low-cost crew may take less money but
perform worse, which can cost more in the long run. Lester always takes 5%.

=== Heist Preparations ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
Heists are unlocked by completing their preparation and setup missions, for example
"Blitz Play" as a setup for the bigger scores. Some preparations require buying
equipment — such as the Carbine Rifles for the Jewel Store Job's Smart approach —
so check the mission briefing and shop at Ammu-Nation before starting. Heist
preparation boards in Lester's garment factory planning room show the selected
approach, crew, and cuts before you confirm the job.
