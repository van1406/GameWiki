# GTA 5 — Weapons

Overview of the main weapon categories available in Grand Theft Auto V.

=== Melee Weapons ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Weapons
Melee weapons include the Fist, Knife, Bat, Crowbar, Hammer, Golf Club, and Pool Cue.
They are silent but require getting close to the target.

=== Pistols ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Weapons
Pistol: The standard sidearm available from the start. Moderate damage and fire rate.
Pistol .50 (Combat Pistol): Higher damage than the standard pistol.
Heavy Pistol: Semi-automatic with a large magazine, good for mid-range fights.
Vintage Pistol: A stylish pistol with lower damage but higher accuracy.
AP Pistol: Fully automatic pistol, unlocked after completing certain missions and
required for drive-by shooting during The Jewel Store Job setups.

=== Submachine Guns ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Weapons
Micro SMG: Compact and fully automatic, effective while driving.
SMG: More accurate and more damaging than the Micro SMG, with a larger magazine.
Assault SMG: Higher rate of fire and magazine capacity, unlocked at higher rank.

=== Shotguns ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Weapons
Pump Shotgun: Standard pump-action shotgun with high close-range damage.
Sawed-Off Shotgun: Double-barreled shotgun that can be fired one in each hand.
Heavy Shotgun: Mag-fed shotgun with extended range.
Musket: Single-shot historical weapon available as a special item.

=== Assault Rifles ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Weapons
Assault Rifle: Reliable fully automatic rifle for medium-range combat.
Carbine Rifle: Accurate and versatile, it is the rifle used in the Smart approach of
The Jewel Store Job; the player must buy it from Ammu-Nation before the heist.
Special Carbine: Heavy rifle with high damage, unlocked later in the game.
Bullpup Rifle: Compact rifle with fast fire rate, acquired during story missions.

=== Sniper Rifles ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Weapons
Sniper Rifle: Bolt-action rifle for long-range headshots.
Heavy Sniper: Higher damage that can destroy vehicles in a few shots.
Marksman Rifle: Semi-automatic rifle for fast follow-up shots at range.

=== Heavy Weapons ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Weapons
RPG (Rocket Launcher): Fires homing or standard rockets; destroys vehicles quickly.
Grenade Launcher: Launches grenades at medium range.
Minigun: Fully automatic heavy gun with very high fire rate.
Grenades and Sticky Bombs: Throwable explosives for ambushes and vehicle kills.
Railgun: Extremely powerful single-shot weapon available in the enhanced edition.

=== Throwables and Utility ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Weapons
Flashbangs, Molotov Cocktails, Pipe Bombs, and Tear Gas are throwable items.
The Parachute and Fire Extinguisher are utility items stored in the inventory.

=== Buying Weapons and Ammo ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
How do I get weapons and ammunition? Buy weapons and ammo at Ammu-Nation stores
(marked with a gun icon on the map); stock unlocks as the story progresses. Ammo
is bought per weapon and also found as pickups or dropped by enemies. Attachments
— scopes, suppressors, extended clips, flashlights, tints — are purchased at the
counter and change accuracy, range, and magazine size. Missions may also drop
weapon pickups; your current loadout carries into every mission.

=== Weapon Upgrades ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
Weapons can be customized at Ammu-Nation with scopes, suppressors, extended clips,
flashlights, and tints. Attachments change accuracy, range, and magazine size.
Ammu-Nation stores are marked on the map and unlock more weapons as the story
progresses.
