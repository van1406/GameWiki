# GTA 5 — Gameplay How-To

Practical gameplay answers: switching characters, special abilities, weapons,
vehicles, money, and using the map.

=== Switching Characters ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
How do I switch characters in GTA 5? Hold down on the D-pad (the character switch
wheel appears in the bottom corner), highlight the portrait of Michael, Franklin,
or Trevor with the left stick, and release. The camera flies to that character and
you take control of them wherever they are on the map. Switching is available
anytime outside missions, and switching away from a wanted character also clears
that character's wanted level. Each protagonist lives in a different part of the
city — Michael in Rockford Hills, Franklin in South Los Santos, Trevor in Sandy
Shores.

=== Special Abilities ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
What is each character's special ability and when can I use it?
- Michael's Bullet Time slows time while aiming, making headshots easy.
- Trevor's Red Mist reduces the damage he takes and increases the damage he deals
  while enraged.
- Franklin's Driving Focus slows time while driving, letting him steer through
  tight gaps at speed.
Each ability runs on a character-colored meter (blue for Michael, green for Trevor,
yellow for Franklin) that fills by driving well, getting headshots, or taking
damage. Activate the ability with the special-ability button (both thumbsticks
pressed together on console controllers) whenever the meter has charge; it lasts
until the meter empties and then recharges while unused.

=== Changing Weapons ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
How do I change weapons? Open the weapon wheel with the weapon-wheel button (hold
L1 on PlayStation, LB on Xbox, or the wheel key on PC) and pick a weapon with the
stick or mouse — the game slows down while the wheel is open. Weapons you own are
grouped into slots: melee, pistols, SMGs, shotguns, rifles, snipers, heavy, and
throwables. On missions you automatically carry your current loadout.

=== Getting Weapons ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
How do I get weapons? Weapons are bought at Ammu-Nation stores, which are marked
with a gun icon on the map and unlock more stock as the story progresses. You also
pick up weapons and ammunition dropped by enemies, and missions often supply
weapon pickups. Higher-tier weapons such as the Carbine Rifle and sniper rifles
become purchasable once their missions unlock them.

=== Ammunition ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
How does ammunition work? Every gun has its own ammo pool, shown next to the weapon
in the wheel. Ammo is bought at Ammu-Nation (buying for a weapon you own), found as
pickups around the map, and dropped by enemies. Throwables like grenades and
Molotovs are limited and restocked at Ammu-Nation. Ammo has a maximum cap per
weapon, so stock up before heists and big firefights.

=== Weapon Categories ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
What weapons are available in GTA 5, and which categories exist? Melee, Pistols, Submachine Guns, Shotguns,
Assault Rifles, Sniper Rifles, Heavy Weapons (RPG, grenade launcher, minigun), and
Throwables (grenades, sticky bombs, Molotovs, tear gas). Each category has its own
slot on the weapon wheel, and Ammu-Nation sells attachments — scopes, suppressors,
extended clips — that change accuracy, range, and magazine size.

=== Getting a Vehicle ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
How do I get a vehicle? The simplest way is to take any parked or moving car on the
street (stealing with witnesses around raises a wanted level). Your personal
vehicle is delivered by the mechanic when you call from the phone. Vehicles can
also be bought from websites on the in-game internet — Legendary Motorsport for
sports cars, Southern San Andreas Super Autos for everyday cars, and Warstock Cache
& Carry for military vehicles — with delivery to your garage.

=== Storing Vehicles ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
How do I store vehicles? Drive a car into your safehouse garage and it is saved as
a personal vehicle, replacing any spot it takes. Personal vehicles with insurance
can be replaced by the mechanic if destroyed. Garage space is limited at each
property, so keep the cars you care about and sell or replace the rest at Los Santos
Customs.

=== Customizing Vehicles ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
How do I customize vehicles? Drive into a Los Santos Customs shop (marked on the
map) to upgrade with new engine, brakes, transmission, turbo, suspension, armor,
windows, paint jobs, horns, lights, and plates. Upgrades cost cash and some parts
unlock as you progress. Customization improves handling, speed, and durability and
applies permanently to that personal vehicle.

=== Finding Specific Vehicles ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
How do I find a specific vehicle? Buy most cars from the in-game websites for
direct delivery. Vehicles that are not for sale can be found spawning in the parts
of the city where that type belongs — sports cars around Rockford Hills and the
marina, muscle cars in South Los Santos, off-road vehicles in Blaine County, and
aircraft at Los Santos International Airport and Sandy Shores Airfield. Military
vehicles like the P-996 LAZER are at Fort Zancudo, where taking one raises a
four-star wanted level instantly. The mechanic also delivers your stored personal
vehicles anywhere on the map.

=== Using the Map and GPS ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
Where is a location on the map? Open the pause-menu map, move the cursor to any
point, and set a waypoint to draw a GPS route on your minimap. Mission markers show
as colored blips, Ammu-Nation as a gun, Los Santos Customs as a spray can, and
property purchases as dollar signs. Following the purple GPS line on the minimap is
the fastest way to reach any destination.

=== Earning Money ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
How do I earn money? The biggest payouts come from heists — the Jewel Store Job
through the Big Score pay millions. Story missions and side jobs pay smaller
amounts, strangers & freaks missions give cash rewards, completing stunt jumps and
races pays out, and robbing stores by aiming a weapon at the clerk hands over the
register cash (and may start a wanted level). Properties and businesses generate
passive income over time, and Lester's assassination missions can be played around
the stock market for large investment profits.
