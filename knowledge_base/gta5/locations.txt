# GTA 5 — Locations

A guide to the main regions, landmarks, and points of interest in Grand Theft Auto V.

=== Los Santos ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Location
Type: City (based on Los Angeles)
Los Santos is the main city of GTA 5 and the southern half of the map. It contains the
downtown financial district, beaches, suburbs, and the airport.

=== Rockford Hills ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Location
Type: Upscale district (based on Beverly Hills)
Rockford Hills is the wealthy district north of downtown Los Santos. It contains the
Vangelico jewelry store on Portola Drive, which is the target of The Jewel Store Job
heist, as well as golf clubs and luxury car dealerships.

=== Vinewood ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Location
Type: Entertainment district (based on Hollywood)
Vinewood sits in the hills north of downtown and features the Vinewood sign, the
Vinewood Bowl amphitheater, and many film studios and boulevards.

=== Downtown Los Santos ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Location
Type: Business district
The skyscraper core of Los Santos, home to the Union Depository where the gold vault
of The Big Score is located, and Maze Bank Tower, the tallest building in the city.

=== Vespucci Beach ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Location
Type: Beach (based on Venice Beach)
Vespucci Beach has a long boardwalk, a gym, and a pier with an amusement park.

=== South Los Santos ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Location
Type: Inner-city neighborhood (based on South Central LA)
The starting area for Franklin, home to Grove Street and the gang territories of the
Families and the Ballas.

=== Blaine County ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Location
Type: Rural county (based on Imperial and Riverside Counties)
The northern half of the map, a desert region containing Sandy Shores, Paleto Bay,
the Alamo Sea, and Mount Chiliad.

=== Sandy Shores ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Location
Type: Desert town
A dusty town on the east shore of the Alamo Sea. It is Trevor's home and the base of
Trevor Philips Enterprises.

=== Paleto Bay ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Location
Type: Small coastal town (based on Paleto Bay in GTA San Andreas)
A quiet town on the north coast with a bank that is robbed in The Paleto Score.

=== Mount Chiliad ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Location
Type: Mountain landmark
The tallest peak on the map, located in the northwest of Blaine County. A cable car
and a dirt trail lead to the summit, and a jetpack myth is associated with it.

=== Fort Zancudo ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Location
Type: Military base
A heavily guarded military base in the western hills. Entering it raises a four-star
wanted level immediately; the P-996 LAZER jets are kept here.

=== Los Santos International Airport ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Location
Type: Airport (based on LAX)
LSIA is in the south of Los Santos with long runways used for stealing planes and
racing missions.

=== Sandy Shores Airfield ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Location
Type: Airstrip
A small desert airstrip used by Trevor for smuggling operations.

=== Vangelico Jewelry Store ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Location
Type: Jewelry store
Where is Vangelico? The Vangelico jewelry store is on Portola Drive in Rockford
Hills, the upscale district north of downtown Los Santos (the in-game Beverly
Hills). It is the target of The Jewel Store Job heist — the crew hits it for its
display cases — and the store is marked on the map in Rockford Hills. What is
Vangelico used for? After the heist it reopens as a normal shop selling jewelry.

=== Point of Interest Mechanics ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Game Mechanics
- The in-game internet and phone let the player buy vehicles, properties, and clothes.
- Random encounters and strangers & freaks missions are marked with letter icons on
  the map.
- Stunt jumps are scattered across the map and reward cash when completed.
