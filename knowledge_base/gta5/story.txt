# GTA 5 — Story

The main story of Grand Theft Auto V: three protagonists, one city, and the heists
that tie them together.

=== Story Overview ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Story
Grand Theft Auto V follows three criminals in the city of Los Santos: Michael, a
retired bank robber living in luxury under a secret deal; Trevor, his old partner
in crime running a gun business in the desert; and Franklin, a young hustler
looking to escape gang life. Their stories converge when Michael's past catches up
with him, pulling all three into a series of heists and into conflict with corrupt
FIB agent Steve Haines and billionaire Devin Weston.

=== The Three Protagonists ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Story
Michael De Santa faked his death in a North Yankton bank robbery 19 years ago and
now lives in Rockford Hills with his family, bored and restless. Trevor Philips,
who believed Michael was dead, runs Trevor Philips Enterprises in Sandy Shores
until he discovers the truth. Franklin Clinton leaves the Families gang in South
Los Santos after meeting Michael and becomes a partner in the heist crew. All three
are playable and can be switched at any time.

=== Main Story Arc ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Story
The story moves from the prologue bank robbery to Franklin and Michael's first
heist — the Jewel Store Job — which brings Trevor back into Michael's life. From
there the crew is pulled into jobs for the FIB and Martin Madrazo, including the
Merryweather Heist, Blitz Play, the Paleto Score, and finally the Big Score, while
Trevor hunts for the truth about the past. The threads come to a head when both
Devin Weston and Steve Haines order Franklin to kill a friend, leading to the
final mission's choice of endings.

=== Endings ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Story
The final mission offers three endings. Ending C — Deathwish, the canon ending, has
Michael, Trevor, and Franklin work together to eliminate their blackmailers —
Stretch, Steve Haines, and Devin Weston — and all three protagonists survive. The
alternatives have Franklin kill either Trevor or Michael, permanently removing
that character from the game. Ending C is the recommended choice for keeping the
whole cast alive for free roam.

=== Antagonists ===
Game: GTA 5
Category: Story
The main antagonists are Steve Haines, a corrupt FIB agent who blackmails the crew
into dangerous jobs; Devin Weston, a billionaire who strings Michael along with
promises of wealth; and Stretch, a Franklin's old gang contact working against the
crew. Merryweather Security, the private military company, also repeatedly stands
between the crew and their targets.
