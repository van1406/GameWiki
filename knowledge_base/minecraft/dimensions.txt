# Minecraft — Dimensions

Practical guides to the two dimensions beyond the Overworld: the Nether and the End.

=== Nether ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Dimension
How to enter: Build a portal frame of obsidian — 10 to 14 blocks in a rectangular
frame at least 4 blocks tall and 5 wide (the corner blocks are optional) — and
light the inside with flint and steel. The purple portal teleports you into the
Nether on the other side.
Important locations: Nether fortresses (dark nether-brick structures with bridges
and blaze spawners), Bastion Remnants (piglin fortresses with gold treasure),
lava oceans, glowstone ceilings, and ancient debris buried around Y 8 to Y 22.
Important mobs: Blazes (fortresses), ghasts (open caverns), piglins (hostile
unless you wear gold armor), hoglins, magma cubes, wither skeletons, and zombie
piglins (neutral until attacked).
Resources: Nether quartz, nether gold, glowstone, basalt, nether wart, blaze rods,
soul sand, and ancient debris, which smelts into netherite scrap.
Survival tips: Beds explode in the Nether — never try to sleep. Water buckets do
not place water, so bring fire resistance potions instead. Wear gold armor so
piglins stay neutral, carry flint and steel to relight portals, and bridge carefully
over lava. One block walked in the Nether equals eight blocks in the Overworld, so
Nether travel is very fast.

=== The End ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Dimension
How to enter: Find the Stronghold by throwing eyes of ender (crafted from ender
pearls and blaze powder), which float toward it. Fill the End portal frame with
twelve eyes of ender and jump in.
Important locations: The central island with the exit portal and obsidian pillars
topped by end crystals; the outer islands with End cities, end ships (which hold
elytra), and chorus fruit groves reached through end gateway portals after the
dragon dies.
Important mobs: The Ender Dragon (boss), endermen (neutral until looked at), and
shulkers in End cities, which shoot homing bullets that give Levitation.
Resources: End stone, purpur blocks, chorus fruit, shulker shells, and elytra from
End ships.
Survival tips: Avoid looking at endermen or wear a carved pumpkin; bring blocks to
bridge between pillars, a bow for the dragon and crystals, and plenty of food.
Dragon breath can be collected in glass bottles for lingering potions.

=== Nether Fortress ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Dimension
How to find: Nether fortresses generate throughout the Nether — explore open
caverns and lava seas looking for dark nether-brick structures with long bridges
and fenced corridors. They can be far from your portal, so bring extra blocks,
food, fire resistance, and a sword.
Why visit them: Fortresses hold blaze spawners (blaze rods for reaching the End),
nether wart gardens at the base of stairs, wither skeletons on the bridges, and
chests with loot. Blaze rods and nether wart make fortresses mandatory for
progression.
Survival tips: Fight blazes on the bridge with cover from the pillars, block off
wider corridors to limit wither skeleton rushes, and mark your way home with
torches.

=== Bastion Remnants ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Dimension
What it is: Massive blackstone fortresses inhabited by piglins and the far
deadlier piglin brutes — the treasure vaults of the Nether.
Where to find them: Bastions generate in most Nether biomes; their towering
blackstone walls are visible from a distance across open caverns.
Loot: Gold blocks, stacks of gold ingots, diamonds, iron, obsidian, and
netherite upgrade smithing templates found in bastion chests.
Survival tips: Wear gold armor so regular piglins stay neutral (brutes
attack regardless), never mine gold or open chests in their sight, and
approach from above with blocks ready. Bartering gold with piglins inside a
safe room is the fastest way to stockpile ender pearls.

=== Nether Resources and How to Get Them ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Dimension
What does the Nether give you, and how?
- Blaze rods: kill blazes at fortress spawners (craft into blaze powder for
  brewing fuel, eyes of ender, and strength potions).
- Nether wart: harvest the gardens at the bases of fortress stairs.
- Ancient debris: mine at y 8 to 22 with a diamond pickaxe, smelt into
  netherite scrap.
- Quartz and nether gold: mine the orange quartz and gold ore veins for
  experience — quartz crafts comparators and observers, and gold feeds piglin
  bartering.
- Glowstone: break the glowing ceiling clusters (silk touch collects them
  whole) for light and potion potency.
- Soul sand and soul soil: from soul sand valleys — slows movement, grows
  nether wart, and spawns the Wither with skulls.
- Basalt and blackstone: from basalt deltas and basalt biomes — blast-proof
  building blocks.
Practical tip: Every Nether run should return with rods, wart, and a few
pieces of ancient debris.

=== End Cities and the Elytra ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Dimension
Where to find them: After the Ender Dragon falls, an end gateway portal
opens on the island's edge — throw an ender pearl (or bridge) through it to
reach the outer islands, then look for tall purpur towers on the horizon.
What they contain: Shulker-guarded chests with enchanted diamond gear,
iron, diamonds, emeralds, and experience; end ships moored beside the towers
hold the elytra in an item frame.
Why go: The elytra changes the game — pair it with firework rockets for
unlimited flight, and collect shulker shells for portable shulker boxes.
Survival tips: Bridge with a wall between islands so endermen cannot knock
you off, carry slow falling for levitation safety, and fight shulkers from
range behind cover.

=== The End Portal ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Dimension
How to find: Craft eyes of ender from four ender pearls and four blaze powder,
then throw one — it flies toward the Stronghold. Follow until it stops going
sideways, dig down, and find the portal room.
How to activate: Place an eye of ender in each of the twelve frame slots; when all
twelve are filled, the black portal activates and you can jump into the End.
Tips: Bring twelve eyes of ender at minimum plus extra pearls to throw; the portal
room's silverfish spawner can be destroyed before it causes trouble.
