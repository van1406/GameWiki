# Minecraft — Tools and Weapons

Every tool and weapon type: how to craft it, how it performs, durability, and
how the tool progression works.

=== Tool Tiers and Progression ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Tools and Weapons
Tool tiers from lowest to highest: wood, stone, iron, golden, diamond,
netherite. Progression: punch a tree for wood → wooden pickaxe → stone →
mine coal and iron → iron tools → find diamonds → diamond tools → upgrade to
netherite at a smithing table. Each higher tier mines faster, lasts longer,
and unlocks harder blocks. Wooden tools come from planks, stone tools from
cobblestone, iron from iron ingots, diamond from diamonds; netherite tools are
made by upgrading diamond tools, not from scratch.

=== Pickaxes ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Tools and Weapons
Materials: 3 tool-tier items (planks, cobblestone, iron ingots, diamonds) + 2
sticks.
Recipe: Three material blocks across the top row, two sticks stacked in the
middle column below them.
How to Craft: In a crafting table, fill the top row with the material, then
place sticks in the center and bottom-center slots.
Uses: The pickaxe mines stone, ores, and most hard blocks. Its tier decides
which ores drop items, so the pickaxe is the single most important tool.
Tips: Keep a Silk Touch pickaxe for ore blocks and a Fortune pickaxe for
breaking them later.

=== Axes ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Tools and Weapons
Materials: 3 tool-tier items + 2 sticks.
Recipe: Material blocks in the top-left corner pattern with two sticks below.
How to Craft: Place three material blocks in the top-left corner of the
crafting grid (two in the top row, one under the left one) and sticks in the
middle and bottom-center slots.
Uses: Chops logs and wooden blocks much faster than any other tool, strips
bark from logs for decorative wood, and doubles as a heavy-hitting weapon with
slow swings. Axes also mine their tier of copper-type blocks quickly.
Tips: A stone axe in the early game out-damages most swords; repair axes with
the same material on an anvil.

=== Shovels ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Tools and Weapons
Materials: 1 tool-tier item + 2 sticks.
Recipe: One material block on top of two stacked sticks in the middle column.
How to Craft: Put the material in the top-center slot and sticks in the center
and bottom-center slots.
Uses: Digs dirt, sand, gravel, clay, snow, and grass paths quickly. Shovels
are the fastest tool for terraforming and for moving large amounts of sand or
gravel.
Tips: A shovel with Efficiency turns digging into instant work; keep one for
sand temples and ocean floors.

=== Hoes ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Tools and Weapons
Materials: 2 tool-tier items + 2 sticks.
Recipe: Two material blocks in the top-left with two sticks below.
How to Craft: Place the two materials in the top-left and top-center slots,
sticks in the center and bottom-center slots.
Uses: Tills dirt or grass blocks into farmland for crops, and mines
farmland-related blocks such as leaves and hay bales quickly.
Tips: A hoe is the first tool of every farm — craft one as soon as you have a
crafting table and spare cobblestone.

=== Swords ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Tools and Weapons
Materials: 2 sword-tier items (planks, cobblestone, iron ingots, gold ingots,
diamonds) + 1 stick.
Recipe: Two material blocks stacked vertically in the middle column, one stick
below them.
How to Craft: Open a crafting table, place the two materials in the two upper
middle slots, the stick in the bottom middle slot, and take the sword.
Uses: The main melee weapon. Damage by tier (Java, before enchantments):
wooden and golden 4, stone 5, iron 6, diamond 7, netherite 8 attack damage.
Enchant with Sharpness for general damage, Smite for undead, Looting for extra
drops, Fire Aspect to set targets alight, and Knockback for space.
Tips: In Java combat, land full-strength swings (full cooldown) instead of
spamming — each timed hit does maximum damage.

=== Bows ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Tools and Weapons
Materials: 3 sticks + 3 string.
Recipe: Sticks and string in a diagonal arc pattern.
How to Craft: Arrange three sticks and three string in the bow pattern (sticks
on the outer curve, string across) and take the bow.
Uses: Ranged weapon — hold to draw, release to fire arrows. Enchant with
Power for damage, Flame to ignite arrows, Punch for knockback, and Infinity to
consume no arrows (Infinity cannot be combined with Mending).
Tips: Keep distance from creepers and phantoms and lead fast-moving targets
slightly. Crafting an arrow needs one flint, one stick, and one feather.

=== Crossbows ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Tools and Weapons
Materials: Crafted from sticks, string, and an iron ingot.
Recipe: Built in a crafting table with an iron ingot in the middle and sticks
and string around it.
How to Craft: Open a crafting table and arrange the sticks, string, and iron
ingot in the crossbow pattern shown in the recipe book.
Uses: Loads a bolt (arrow, firework rocket, or speckled arrow) that stays
loaded until fired, dealing heavy single-shot damage. Enchant with Multishot
to fire three projectiles, Quick Charge to load faster, and Piercing to hit
several mobs in a line.
Tips: Crossbows fire fireworks for long-range elytra-free attacks and are the
best weapon when you need one powerful opening shot.

=== Shields ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Tools and Weapons
Materials: 6 planks + 1 iron ingot.
Recipe: Y-shaped pattern with the iron ingot at the top center.
How to Craft: Iron ingot in the top-center slot with a plank on each side, a
full middle row of planks, and one plank in the bottom-center slot.
Uses: Hold the use button to block incoming melee, arrow, and most projectile
damage from the front. A banner can be attached for decoration.
Tips: Shields block skeleton arrows, ghast fireballs, and axe hits, and they
are the best defense against creepers at close range — back away while
blocking. Attacking while blocking with an axe can disable an enemy shield in
PvP.

=== Tridents ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Tools and Weapons
What it is: A powerful melee and ranged weapon that can only be obtained as a
drop — it is not craftable.
Where it comes from: Drowned zombies occasionally carry one and drop it when
killed; tridents can also be found in buried treasure and ocean ruin chests.
Uses: Deals heavy melee damage and, when thrown, flies as a ranged projectile
that hits mobs and can trigger lightning rods; it also works underwater.
Enchant with Loyalty to return it to you, Channeling to summon lightning in
storms, Riptide to launch yourself in rain or water, and Impaling to deal extra
damage to aquatic mobs.
Tips: Keep Unbreaking and Mending on a trident — they are rare. Riptide
tridents are the fastest water transportation in the game.

=== Durability and Repair ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Tools and Weapons
Durability ranks (weakest to strongest): golden, wooden, stone, iron, diamond,
netherite — golden gear is fast but breaks almost immediately. Every use chips
one durability point; when durability hits zero the item breaks.
How to repair: Combine the damaged item with its material (or an identical
item) on an anvil, or enchant it with Unbreaking to slow wear and Mending to
repair it automatically using experience orbs.
Practical tips: Carry a spare pickaxe on long mining trips, put Unbreaking
and Mending on diamond gear, and avoid using golden tools outside the Nether.

=== Combat Basics ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Tools and Weapons
How do I fight mobs effectively?
- Keep the shield in your off-hand: block first, strike during the opening.
- In Java combat, wait for the attack cooldown to reload between sword swings
  for full damage.
- Use the bow or crossbow for anything that flies or keeps distance.
- Enchant gear before hard fights: Sharpness, Protection, and Unbreaking make
  a huge difference.
- Bread, cooked meat, and golden apples keep health up mid-fight; healing
  potions restore instantly.
- Run and reset the fight when overwhelmed — losing all your gear at death is
  far worse than retreating.
