# Minecraft — Crafting

Crafting recipes and crafting system basics for Minecraft. Each recipe lists its
materials, the pattern, how to craft it, and what it is used for.

=== Crafting Table ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Crafting
Materials: 4 planks (any wood type)
Recipe: 2x2 square of planks filling all four slots of the crafting grid
How to Craft: Open the 2x2 grid in your inventory, place one plank in each of the
four slots, and take the crafting table from the result slot.
Uses: Place the table on the ground and use it to open the 3x3 crafting grid that
unlocks most recipes in the game.

=== Torch ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Crafting
Materials: 1 stick + 1 coal or charcoal
Recipe: Coal (or charcoal) directly above a stick
How to Craft: Place the coal above the stick in the crafting grid (any grid works —
the recipe is shapeless) and take the result; each craft makes 4 torches.
Uses: Torches provide a light level of 14 that prevents hostile mob spawns in dark
areas, lighting caves, houses, and tunnels. They can also be placed on floors and
walls as decoration.

=== Chest ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Crafting
Materials: 8 planks
Recipe: Ring of planks around the edge of the 3x3 grid with an empty center
How to Craft: Fill every slot of the middle and outer rows except the center slot
with planks, then take the chest.
Uses: A chest stores 27 slots of items. Two chests placed side by side merge into
a large chest with 54 slots.

=== Furnace ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Crafting
Materials: 8 cobblestone
Recipe: Ring of cobblestone around the edge of the 3x3 grid with an empty center
How to Craft: Fill the outer ring of the grid with cobblestone, leave the center
empty, and take the furnace.
Uses: The furnace smelts ore into ingots and cooks food. It burns fuel such as
coal, charcoal, planks, or lava buckets.

=== Iron Ingot ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Crafting
Materials: 1 iron ore + fuel (coal, charcoal, or planks)
Recipe: Not crafted in a grid — smelted in a furnace
How to Craft: Place iron ore in the top slot of the furnace and fuel in the bottom
slot; the smelted iron ingot appears in the result slot.
Uses: Iron ingots are the base material for tools, armor, buckets, minecarts,
shields, and anvils.

=== Diamond Sword ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Crafting
Materials: 2 diamonds + 1 stick
Recipe: Two diamonds stacked vertically in the middle column, one stick below them
How to Craft: Open a crafting table, place the diamonds in the two upper middle
slots, put the stick in the bottom middle slot, and take the sword.
Uses: The diamond sword deals 7 attack damage (Java Edition, before enchantments)
and is the main melee weapon for fighting mobs and bosses. It can be enchanted with
Sharpness, Smite, Looting, and Unbreaking.

=== Diamond Pickaxe ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Crafting
Materials: 3 diamonds + 2 sticks
Recipe: Three diamonds across the top row, two sticks stacked in the middle column
below the center diamond
How to Craft: In a crafting table, fill the top row with diamonds, place sticks in
the center and bottom-center slots, and take the pickaxe.
Uses: A diamond pickaxe mines obsidian and is required to reach the Nether portal
loop efficiently; it also mines diamond and ancient debris ore and lasts longer
than iron.

=== Iron Sword ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Crafting
Materials: 2 iron ingots + 1 stick
Recipe: Two iron ingots stacked vertically in the middle column, one stick below
How to Craft: Place the ingots in the two upper middle slots of the crafting table,
the stick in the bottom middle slot, and take the sword.
Uses: The iron sword deals 6 attack damage and is the reliable mid-game weapon
before you find diamonds.

=== Shield ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Crafting
Materials: 6 planks + 1 iron ingot
Recipe: Top row — plank, iron ingot, plank; middle row — three planks; bottom row —
one plank in the center slot (a Y shape with the iron ingot at the top)
How to Craft: Open a crafting table, put the iron ingot in the top-center slot with
a plank on each side, fill the middle row with three planks, place one plank in the
bottom-center slot, and take the shield.
Uses: Holding right-click (use) blocks incoming melee and arrow damage from the
front. It is the best defense against skeletons and creepers at close range.

=== Bow ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Crafting
Materials: 3 sticks + 3 string
Recipe: Sticks and string arranged in a diagonal arc (stick-string-stick in the
left and right columns with string in the middle)
How to Craft: In a crafting table, arrange three sticks and three string in the
bow pattern shown in the recipe book, then take the bow.
Uses: A ranged weapon that shoots arrows; it can be enchanted with Power, Infinity,
Flame, and Punch. Infinity makes arrows effectively endless.

=== Bed ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Crafting
Materials: 3 wool + 3 planks
Recipe: Wool across the top row, planks across the middle row
How to Craft: Open a crafting table, place wool in the three top slots, planks in
the three middle slots, and take the bed; the wool must all be the same color.
Uses: Beds set the spawn point and skip the night when slept in. Sleeping fails if
hostile mobs are nearby. Beds explode in the Nether and the End.

=== TNT ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Crafting
Materials: 5 gunpowder + 4 sand
Recipe: Alternating gunpowder and sand in a checkerboard pattern
How to Craft: In a crafting table, place gunpowder in the four corners and the
center slots, sand in the remaining slots, and take the TNT.
Uses: TNT is detonated with flint and steel or a redstone signal for mining blasts
and traps. Gunpowder drops from creepers.

=== Stick ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Crafting
Materials: 2 planks
Recipe: Two planks stacked vertically
How to Craft: Place one plank above another in any crafting grid and take the four
sticks.
Uses: Sticks are the handle for nearly every tool, weapon, and torch recipe.

=== Leather Armor ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Crafting
Materials: 7 leather for a full set (helmet 5, chestplate 8, leggings 7,
boots 4 per piece pattern)
Recipe: Helmet = U-shape of 5 leather; chestplate = 8 leather (gap at the
top center); leggings = 7 leather; boots = 4 leather in two columns
How to Craft: Open a crafting table and arrange the leather in the armor
shape shown in the recipe book for each piece.
Uses: The first wearable armor set — weak, but craftable on day one, and it
can be dyed any color.

=== Iron Armor ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Crafting
Materials: 24 iron ingots for a full set
Recipe: Same shapes as leather armor — helmet 5, chestplate 8, leggings 7,
boots 4 iron ingots
How to Craft: Smelt iron ore into ingots, then form each piece in the
crafting table.
Uses: 15 armor points (60% damage reduction) across the set — the practical
mid-game armor that should carry you to diamonds.

=== Diamond Armor ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Crafting
Materials: 24 diamonds for a full set
Recipe: Helmet 5, chestplate 8, leggings 7, boots 4 diamonds in the standard
armor patterns
How to Craft: Mine diamonds below y 16 with an iron pickaxe or better, then
craft each piece.
Uses: 20 armor points (80% damage reduction) with armor toughness — the
standard endgame set, upgradable to netherite at a smithing table.

=== Bucket ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Crafting
Materials: 3 iron ingots
Recipe: Three iron ingots in a V shape (top-left, top-right, bottom-center)
How to Craft: Open a crafting table and place the ingots in the V pattern.
Uses: Carries water or lava — place water to swim up vertical shafts, turn
lava into obsidian, put out fires, and rescue yourself from falls. The
single most valuable early-game tool: craft a bucket as soon as you have
three iron.

=== Flint and Steel ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Crafting
Materials: 1 iron ingot + 1 flint
Recipe: Iron ingot and flint together in any two slots
How to Craft: Dig gravel until flint drops (about a 10% chance per block),
then combine it with an iron ingot.
Uses: Lights Nether portals, fires, TNT, campfires, and candles —
indispensable for the first Nether trip and for lighting builds.

=== Shears ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Crafting
Materials: 2 iron ingots
Recipe: Two iron ingots diagonal from each other
How to Craft: Place two iron ingots in a diagonal (top-left and center, or
top-right and center) in the crafting table.
Uses: Cuts leaves, wool, vines, tall grass, and mushrooms without destroying
them; strips logs; harvests cocoa and nether wart quickly.

=== Brewing Stand ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Crafting
Materials: 1 blaze rod + 3 cobblestone
Recipe: Blaze rod in the top-center slot, cobblestone across the bottom row
How to Craft: The blaze rod comes from blazes in Nether fortresses — the
stand itself is cheap once you have one rod.
Uses: The station for every potion in the game — it brews from water
bottles and ingredients, using blaze powder as fuel.

=== Anvil ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Crafting
Materials: 3 iron blocks + 4 iron ingots
Recipe: Three iron blocks across the top row, three ingots below with one in
the center of the middle row (anvil shape)
How to Craft: Craft nine iron ingots into blocks at a crafting table first,
then form the anvil.
Uses: Combines enchanted items and books, repairs gear with materials, and
renames items — the workshop for perfecting gear.

=== Bookshelf ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Crafting
Materials: 3 books + 6 planks
Recipe: Planks on the top and bottom rows, three books across the middle row
How to Craft: Books need leather (cows) and paper (sugar cane); craft three,
then combine with planks.
Uses: Fifteen arranged around an enchanting table unlock level-30 enchants —
bookshelves are the backbone of any enchanting room.

=== Hopper ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Crafting
Materials: 5 iron ingots + 1 chest
Recipe: Iron ingots at top-left, top-right, and center, with the chest in
the bottom-center slot (a V pouring into the chest)
How to Craft: Sneak-click to aim a hopper at the container it should feed.
Uses: Moves items between containers — under farms for collection, above
furnaces for automatic smelting, and in item sorters.

=== Boat ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Crafting
Materials: 5 planks of the same wood type
Recipe: Boat shape — three planks across the bottom row with one plank above
each end
How to Craft: In a crafting table, place the planks in the boat pattern from
the recipe book.
Uses: The basic water transport — cross oceans safely, reach shipwrecks, and
carry one passenger.

=== Minecart ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Crafting
Materials: 5 iron ingots
Recipe: U shape — three ingots across the bottom row with one above each end
How to Craft: Arrange the ingots in the U pattern in a crafting table.
Uses: Rides on rails; paired with rails and powered rails it forms minecart
lines, elevators, and item transport systems.

=== Flint ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Crafting
What it is: Flint is a drop from gravel blocks, not a crafted item.
How to obtain: Dig or shovel gravel repeatedly — each block has a chance to
drop flint instead of gravel; Fortune does not affect it.
Uses: Crafted with an iron ingot into flint and steel for portals and fires.

=== Crafting Table Tips ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Game Mechanics
- The recipe book (green icon) shows available recipes and can auto-fill patterns.
- Shaped recipes require the exact pattern; shapeless recipes accept any arrangement.
- Some recipes require specific tool tiers: iron pickaxe or better for diamonds,
  diamond or netherite pickaxe for obsidian.
- How do I craft an item? Open a crafting table, check the recipe book for the
  recipe, place the materials in the pattern, and take the result.
