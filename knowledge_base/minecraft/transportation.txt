# Minecraft — Transportation

Boats, minecarts, horses, Nether travel, and elytra — the ways to get around
and when to use each one.

=== Boats ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Transportation
Materials: Wooden planks of a matching wood type.
How to Craft: Five planks in a boat shape (two rows with the ends raised) in
a crafting table — the recipe book shows the pattern.
How to use: Place the boat in water, right-click to ride, and row with the
movement keys; boats carry you and one passenger, and they also work on land,
just slowly.
Uses: Crossing oceans, exploring shipwrecks, and — packed with blue ice —
the fastest ground travel in the game (ice-boat roads beat every other
overland method).
Tips: Craft a boat before your first ocean trip; a boat also saves you from
drowning when your health is low in deep water.

=== Minecarts and Rails ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Transportation
Materials: Minecart = 5 iron ingots in a U shape; rails = iron ingots and
sticks; powered rails = gold ingots, sticks, and redstone; detector rails and
activator rails add redstone behavior.
How to use: Place rails on solid blocks, set minecarts down, and push to
ride. Powered rails need a redstone signal (lever or torch) to boost carts;
place one roughly every 8-9 powered rails on flat ground to keep speed.
Uses: Minecart elevators, item transport lines with hopper minecarts, mob
farms, and base-to-base rail links.
Tips: A minecart with furnace (crafted from a furnace and a minecart) burns
coal to pull itself and other carts along a line. Build stations with a
powered rail at the stop so the cart launches on the way out.

=== Horses and Mounts ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Transportation
How to obtain: Horses spawn in plains and savannas; tame one by repeatedly
riding it until it accepts you (hearts appear), then equip a saddle and,
optionally, horse armor.
Behavior: Horses vary in speed, jump height, and health — keep the fast,
high-jumping ones. Donkeys carry a chest for extra storage, llamas carry
carpets and can be led in caravans, and pigs can be ridden with a carrot on a
stick.
Uses: Fast, fuel-free Overworld travel; horses ignore roads and climb hills.
Tips: Always keep a saddle — it is the only item that makes a horse
controllable, and it can be moved between mounts.

=== Nether Travel ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Transportation
How does Nether travel work? One block in the Nether equals eight blocks in
the Overworld, so a short Nether trip covers huge Overworld distances.
How to do it safely: Build an enclosed highway from your portal — walls and a
roof keep ghasts out, and a floor of packed or blue ice with a boat (or
soul speed boots on soul sand) makes you extremely fast. Place your return
portal at the matching coordinates to the destination so both portals link
directly.
Tips: Carry fire resistance potions, a flint and steel for relighting portals,
and mark tunnels with cobblestone so you never lose the route.

=== Elytra and Firework Rockets ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Transportation
How to get one: Elytra come from the end ship moored next to End cities after
the Ender Dragon falls — bridge through an end gateway, spot the ship, and
take the wings from their item frame.
How to fly: Equip the elytra in the chestplate slot, jump from a height, and
press jump again to glide; use a firework rocket (crafted from paper,
gunpowder, and optionally dyes) while gliding to boost speed and climb.
Uses: The fastest and most flexible travel in the game — base hopping,
exploring, and quick returns from the Nether or caves.
Tips: Bring Mending and Unbreaking on the wings, keep a stack of rockets in
your hotbar, and land on water or use slow falling when your boost runs out.

=== Choosing a Transportation Method ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Transportation
Which travel method should I use?
- Early game: walk and boat — boats for any water crossing.
- Mid game: horses for speed across land, rails for fixed base links.
- Nether phase: an ice-boat highway or powered rail tunnel through the Nether
  for continent-scale fast travel.
- End game: elytra with firework rockets — fly anywhere, carry everything.
Practical tip: Whichever you pick, keep a spare boat and a bed in your
inventory: water and night are the two things that stop every journey.
