# Minecraft — Structures and Locations

Every major structure: what it contains, how to find it, and why it is worth
the trip.

=== Villages ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Structure
What it contains: Houses with beds and chests of food, iron, saplings, and
emeralds; farming plots; workstation blocks; villagers; and a defending iron
golem.
How to find: Villages generate in plains, savannas, deserts, taigas, snowy
plains, and meadows — ride or walk across open land and look for paths and
lamp posts.
Why it is useful: The best early-game stop — free food and beds, a trading
hub, job sites for professions, and a safe place to sleep through the night.

=== Strongholds ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Structure
What it contains: A stone-brick maze with storerooms, a library full of books
and chests, and — on the deepest level — the portal room holding the End
portal, a silverfish spawner, and a lava pool.
How to find: Craft eyes of ender from ender pearls and blaze powder, throw
one, and follow it until it stops drifting sideways; dig down at that point.
The stronghold spawns in rings around the world spawn, so it can be thousands
of blocks away — bring food and a water bucket for the journey.
Why it is useful: It is the only way to reach the End and fight the Ender
Dragon.

=== Nether Fortresses ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Structure
What it contains: Long nether-brick bridges and corridors with blaze spawners,
nether wart gardens at the bases of stairs, wither skeletons, loot chests, and
soul sand patches.
How to find: Fortresses generate throughout the Nether; travel in straight
lines from your portal across open caverns and watch for the dark brick
silhouette against the orange terrain.
Why it is useful: Blaze rods (needed for blaze powder, eyes of ender, brewing
stands, and ender pearls) and nether wart come almost exclusively from
fortresses, making them mandatory for progression.

=== Bastion Remnants ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Structure
What it contains: Blackstone fortress ruins inhabited by piglins and piglin
brutes, with treasure rooms full of gold blocks, gold coins, diamond and
netherite-tier loot, and netherite upgrade smithing templates.
How to find: Bastions generate in most Nether biomes; look for massive
blackstone walls and bridges, especially in nether wastes and crimson forests.
Why it is useful: The richest loot in the Nether and the source of netherite
upgrade templates — but piglin brutes attack on sight no matter what you
wear. Wear gold armor for the regular piglins, never mine gold in front of
them, and bridge around the treasure rooms rather than rushing in.

=== Woodland Mansions ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Structure
What it contains: A huge multi-floor dark-oak mansion filled with vindicators
and evokers, hidden rooms, wool mazes, and chests with emeralds and totems.
How to find: Buy a woodland explorer map from a cartographer villager and
follow the marked forest location — mansions generate far from spawn in dark
forests.
Why it is useful: Evokers drop totems of undying (a second chance at death),
and the mansion holds large emerald loot — but the vindicators deal massive
damage, so bring diamond gear and a good shield.

=== Ocean Monuments ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Structure
What it contains: A prismarine temple guarded by guardians and three elder
guardians, with sea lanterns, prismarine blocks, sponge rooms, and a hidden
core of gold blocks.
How to find: Buy an ocean explorer map from a cartographer villager and dive
at the marked spot — monuments generate in deep oceans.
Why it is useful: Sea lanterns, prismarine, wet sponges, and a hidden core of
gold blocks — guardians drop prismarine shards and crystals used to craft sea
lanterns. Elder guardians inflict Mining Fatigue, so kill them first or mine
nothing at all. Bring water breathing potions and a solid sword.

=== Ancient Cities ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Structure
What it contains: A massive dark city of deepslate at the bottom of the
Overworld (around y -50), built around sculk growth, loot chests containing
swift sneak books, echo shards, and disc fragments, and a central portal
structure.
How to find: Ancient cities generate deep underground beneath mountain and
plains regions — dig down where the surface tells you the deep dark lies, or
follow cave systems to the lowest levels.
Why it is useful: Unique loot you cannot get anywhere else — but sculk
shriekers summon the Warden, which senses vibrations and kills in one or two
hits. Sneak everywhere, and walk on wool blocks or carpet paths since wool
muffles your footsteps from the Warden's senses. If the Warden rises, run;
it is meant to be survived, not fought.

=== Desert Temples ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Structure
What it contains: A sandstone pyramid with a colored terracotta pattern and
four loot chests in a hidden chamber beneath the floor.
How to find: They generate on the surface of desert biomes — spot the orange
and blue pattern above the dunes.
Why it is useful: Chests can hold diamonds, emeralds, and horse armor — but
the floor hides a stone pressure plate on TNT. Drop down the central hole
slowly, mine the TNT or step around the plate, then loot the four side
chests.

=== Jungle Temples ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Structure
What it contains: A mossy stone-brick temple with a lever puzzle that opens a
hidden room, an arrow-trap corridor, and chests with emeralds, diamonds, and
lapis.
How to find: They generate hidden in jungle biomes — watch for mossy stone
peeking through the leaves.
Why it is useful: Free loot plus a fun puzzle; the hidden room opens by
pulling the correct combination of levers on the wall.

=== End Cities ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Structure
What it contains: Tall purpur towers and terraces on the outer End islands,
guarded by shulkers, with chests of enchanted diamond and netherite-tier
loot, iron, diamonds, and shulker shells — and end ships moored nearby that
hold the elytra.
How to find: After defeating the Ender Dragon, an end gateway portal appears
on the island edge; throw an ender pearl or build across into it, then bridge
between outer islands and watch for the purple towers on the horizon.
Why it is useful: The elytra (from the end ship) is the game's best
transportation, and the cities are the main source of enchanted endgame loot.

=== Abandoned Mineshafts ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Structure
What it contains: Wooden-support tunnels with rails, minecart chests holding
rails, melon seeds, iron, and diamonds, plus cobwebs and cave spiders.
How to find: They generate throughout the Overworld underground; exposed
mineshafts also appear on the surface in badlands biomes.
Why it is useful: Easy early loot and a ready-made tunnel network — but cave
spiders poison, so light it up section by section and watch the webs.

=== Trial Chambers ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Structure
What it contains: A copper-decorated underground dungeon with trial spawners,
vaults that dispense loot, dispensers, and the breeze — a wind mob that fires
wind charges.
How to find: Trial chambers generate randomly in the Overworld underground
(added in the 1.21 update); they are not sold on maps, so caving or exploring
deep tunnels finds them.
Why it is useful: Vault loot includes emeralds, diamonds, breeze rods, and
exclusive rewards such as the flow and bolt armor trims; the combat trial is a
good mid-game gear check. Bring a shield for the breeze's wind charges and
food for the spawner waves.

=== How to Find Structures ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Structure
What is the fastest way to find each structure?
- Stronghold, woodland mansion, ocean monument: buy explorer maps from
  cartographer villagers and follow them.
- Villages, ruined portals, desert temples: explore during the day and watch
  the horizon for paths, lamp posts, and sandstone pyramids.
- Nether fortress, bastion: travel in straight lines from your Nether portal
  and mark the path with cobblestone.
- End cities: after the dragon, take the end gateway and bridge outward.
- Trial chambers and ancient cities: dig deep and explore below y 0.
