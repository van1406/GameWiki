# Minecraft — Potions and Brewing

How brewing works, every important potion, and where to find the ingredients.

=== The Brewing Stand ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Potions
Materials: 1 blaze rod + 3 cobblestone.
Recipe: Blaze rod on top, cobblestone across the bottom row.
How to Craft: In a crafting table, place the blaze rod in the top-center
slot and cobblestone in the three bottom slots.
How to use: Place the stand, put blaze powder in the top-left fuel slot, add
water bottles in the bottom slots, and ingredients in the top slot. Each
blaze powder unit fuels several brews. A cauldron is not required — bottles go
straight into the stand.

=== Brewing Basics ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Potions
How does brewing work? Every potion starts the same way:
1. Fill three glass bottles with water (right-click a water source).
2. Add nether wart to the bottles to make the awkward potion — the base for
   almost every effect potion.
3. Add the effect ingredient (glistering melon, sugar, blaze powder, etc.) to
   turn the awkward potion into the finished potion.
4. Modify it: redstone extends duration, glowstone makes it stronger,
   gunpowder makes it a splash potion, and dragon's breath makes it linger.
5. Fermented spider eye corrupts most potions (healing becomes harming,
   swiftness becomes slowness, night vision becomes invisibility).
Practical tips: Always brew in batches of three — one set of bottles makes
three potions from a single ingredient.

=== Base Potions ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Potions
- Water bottle: filled from any water source.
- Awkward potion: water bottle + nether wart; the base of nearly every effect
  potion in the game.
- Mundane and thick potions: other early intermediates that are rarely useful
  on their own — almost every recipe you want starts from awkward.
Practical tips: Keep a chest of awkward potions ready so every new brew only
needs its effect ingredient.

=== Important Potions ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Potions
What are the most useful potions?
- Healing (glistering melon): restores health instantly; splash healing is the
  best mid-fight heal.
- Regeneration (ghast tear): heals over time.
- Strength (blaze powder): adds melee damage — essential for boss fights.
- Swiftness (sugar): faster movement and mining speed.
- Night Vision (golden carrot): see in the dark for eight minutes; add
  fermented spider eye for invisibility.
- Fire Resistance (magma cream): immune to fire and lava — the single most
  important Nether potion.
- Water Breathing (pufferfish): underwater exploration and monuments.
- Slow Falling (phantom membrane): negates fall damage — perfect for the End
  and for elytra travel.
- Poison (spider eye): drains health over time (offensive).
- Leaping (rabbit's foot): jump higher.
- Weakness (fermented spider eye in awkward base): lowers melee damage —
  also used to cure zombie villagers with a splash potion.
Practical tips: Splash versions of healing and strength belong in your hotbar
during every hard fight.

=== Splash, Lingering, and Tipped Arrows ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Potions
- Splash potions: add gunpowder — throw to affect yourself or mobs in an area.
- Lingering potions: add dragon's breath — create a cloud that applies the
  effect to anything inside; dragon's breath is collected with glass bottles
  during the Ender Dragon fight.
- Tipped arrows: craft arrows with lingering potions — shoot the effect from
  range.
Practical tips: Lingering healing clouds let you heal allies (or yourself)
by walking through them, while lingering harming clouds soften a fight before
it starts.

=== How to Obtain Brewing Ingredients ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Potions
Where do I get brewing ingredients?
- Nether wart: grown on soul sand; found in fortress stair gardens and bastion
  chests — the first thing to grab in the Nether.
- Blaze powder: craft blaze rods (dropped by blazes in fortresses); it is also
  the brewing stand fuel.
- Glistering melon: melon slices + gold nuggets.
- Golden carrot: carrot + 8 gold nuggets.
- Magma cream: dropped by magma cubes, or crafted from blaze powder + slimeball.
- Ghast tear and gunpowder: dropped by ghasts.
- Rabbit's foot: dropped by rabbits.
- Pufferfish: caught by fishing or found in warm oceans.
- Phantom membrane: dropped by phantoms.
- Spider eye: dropped by spiders; fermented with brown mushroom and sugar.
- Dragon's breath: collected with glass bottles during the dragon fight.
- Sugar: crafted from sugar cane; redstone, glowstone, and gunpowder come
  from mining, the Nether, and creepers respectively.

=== Practical Brewing Tips ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Potions
- Build a brewing room near your Nether portal with stands, a water source,
  and labeled chests for ingredients.
- Brew Fire Resistance before every Nether trip and Slow Falling before the
  End.
- Splash healing is faster to use in combat than eating — keep two stacks.
- Redstone doubles potion duration at the cost of potency; glowstone
  upgrades potency but shortens duration — pick one, they conflict.
- Witches drop potions and brewing ingredients when killed; their huts are
  free early loot.
- Fermented spider eye (brown mushroom + sugar + spider eye) is the
  corruption ingredient — keep it stocked to flip potions into their reverse
  effects.
