# Minecraft — Enchanting and Repair

The full enchanting system: the table, bookshelves, experience, the important
enchantments, anvils, mending, and a practical enchanting progression.

=== The Enchanting Table ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Enchanting
Materials: 2 diamonds + 4 obsidian + 1 book.
Recipe: Three obsidian across the bottom row; the two diamonds and the fourth
obsidian block in the middle row; the book on top (the in-game recipe book
fills in the exact slots).
How to Craft: In a crafting table, fill the bottom row with obsidian, place
the two diamonds beside the remaining obsidian in the middle row, and put the
book in the top center slot.
How to use: Place the table, open it, put the item to enchant in the left
slot and lapis lazuli in the right slot, then choose one of the three offered
enchantments. Enchanting spends experience levels plus lapis — higher-tier
offers need more lapis and levels.

=== Bookshelves and Level 30 Enchanting ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Enchanting
How do I get level 30 enchants? Surround the enchanting table with 15
bookshelves, each exactly one block away from the table (leave the one-block
air gap — a block between shelf and table breaks the link). The shelves
upgrade the offers up to the maximum level 30. Bookshelves craft from three
books and six planks; books need leather and paper (paper comes from sugar
cane).
Practical tips: Build the classic ring: table on a floor with a one-block
moat of air and shelves one block out, three shelves high along the walls —
that is 14-15 shelves and level 30 enchants.

=== Experience and Levels ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Enchanting
Where does experience come from? Mining ores (coal, lapis, redstone,
diamond, nether quartz), killing mobs, smelting in furnaces, trading with
villagers, breeding animals, and using bottles o' enchanting. Levels are
spent at the enchanting table, on anvils (renaming, combining, repairing),
and on the grindstone returns some back.
Practical tips: mobs and furnaces are the fastest XP; a spawner-based farm
feeds level 30 quickly. You need to reach level 30 to unlock the strongest
offer, but the enchant itself only consumes a few levels — keep enchanting to
keep spending.

=== How Enchanting Works ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Enchanting
How does enchanting work? Put the item and lapis in the enchanting table,
pick one of the three offers, and the item gains that enchantment (plus one or
two bonus enchantments at random). Offers are fixed per item and refresh
after you enchant something. Enchantment outcomes depend on your experience
level and available bookshelves. Some enchantments conflict and cannot coexist
on one item — for example Protection and Fire Protection, or Infinity and
Mending.

=== Important Enchantments ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Enchanting
What are the most important enchantments?
- Tools: Efficiency (mining speed), Unbreaking (durability), Fortune (more
  ore drops), Silk Touch (mine the block itself), Mending (auto-repair).
- Weapons: Sharpness (damage), Smite (undead bonus), Looting (more drops),
  Knockback, Fire Aspect, and on bows Power, Flame, Punch, Infinity.
- Armor: Protection (all damage), Fire/Blast/Projectile Protection
  (specialized), Feather Falling (falls), Thorns (reflect damage), Respiration
  and Aqua Affinity (underwater).
- Trident: Loyalty, Channeling, Riptide, Impaling.
- Crossbow: Multishot, Quick Charge, Piercing.
Conflicts to know: Infinity and Mending cannot share a bow; the different
Protection types cannot stack (except Thorns and Feather Falling, which pair
normally).

=== Anvils and Combining ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Enchanting
How do I combine enchantments? On an anvil (3 iron blocks + 4 iron ingots),
put the main item in the first slot and an enchanted book or a second item in
the second to merge their enchantments, repair the item, or rename it. Higher
enchantments combine into stronger versions (Sharpness I + Sharpness I =
Sharpness II).
Costs: Every anvil use adds a prior-work penalty in levels, so combining
deeply gets expensive; combine in a planned order and keep the total under 30
levels per operation. Renaming costs just one level the first time.
Tips: Use the anvil to place Mending books on your best gear — that single
book outlives every other investment.

=== The Grindstone ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Enchanting
What it does: The grindstone (2 sticks + 1 stone slab + 2 planks) removes
enchantments from an item and refunds some experience, and it repairs two
identical items together. Use it to strip bad table rolls before reusing the
item, or to clean loot gear you plan to re-enchant.

=== Mending and Repair ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Enchanting
What is Mending? Mending consumes experience orbs to repair the item it is on,
up to full durability — combined with Unbreaking, gear effectively never
breaks. Mending cannot be made at an enchanting table: get Mending books from
librarians, chest loot, or fishing.
Repair options: anvil with material, anvil with an identical item, grindstone
combining, or Mending. Experience is best spent on Mending first, then
Unbreaking.
Tips: Put Mending and Unbreaking III on your diamond/netherite set, sword,
pickaxe, and elytra the moment you have the books.

=== Practical Enchantment Progression ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Enchanting
What is the fastest enchanting progression?
1. Mine enough diamonds for two — one for the enchanting table, one spare.
2. Craft a table and 15 bookshelves; grow sugar cane and keep cows for books.
3. Reach level 30 through mining or a mob spawner, then enchant a diamond
   pickaxe first (Efficiency, Unbreaking, or Fortune pays for everything).
4. Enchant armor pieces at 30 until you roll Protection sets.
5. Reroll unwanted enchants through the grindstone and try again.
6. Trade with librarians for Mending, combine on an anvil, and finish with
   Unbreaking III on every piece.
7. After netherite upgrades, re-check gear — upgrades keep all enchantments.
