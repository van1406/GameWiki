# Minecraft — Items

Key items, blocks, and materials in Minecraft.

=== Diamond ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Item
Type: Resource
Diamonds are found only below Y level 16, most often between Y -58 and Y -53, and are
mined with an iron pickaxe or better. They are used for diamond tools, armor, and a
netherite upgrade ingredient via a smithing table.

=== Netherite Ingot ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Item
Type: Resource
Netherite is crafted by combining one netherite scrap with four gold ingots. Netherite
scrap comes from smelting ancient debris, which spawns rarely in the Nether around
Y 8 to Y 22. Netherite tools and armor are stronger than diamond and do not burn in lava.

=== Ender Pearl ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Item
Type: Material
Ender pearls drop from endermen. Throwing one teleports the player a short distance.
Crafting four pearls with four blaze powder creates eyes of ender, which point to the
Stronghold for the End portal.

=== Blaze Rod ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Item
Type: Material
Blaze rods drop from blazes in Nether fortresses. They are fuel for brewing stands and
craft into blaze powder, needed for eyes of ender and to charge an end crystal.

=== Golden Apple ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Item
Type: Food
A golden apple is crafted with eight gold blocks around one apple, or found in chests.
Eating it grants Absorption and Regeneration for a short time, useful in PvP and against
the Wither.

=== Shield ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Item
Type: Tool
Crafted from six planks and one iron ingot. Holding right-click blocks incoming melee
and arrow damage from the front. Banner patterns can be applied to customize shields
in Bedrock Edition.

=== Bow ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Item
Type: Weapon
Crafted from three sticks and three string. Arrows are crafted from flint, a stick, and
a feather. A bow can be enchanted with Power, Infinity, Flame, and Punch.

=== Iron Pickaxe ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Item
Type: Tool
Crafted from three iron ingots and two sticks. It is the minimum tier needed to mine
diamond ore, and it mines most stone blocks quickly.

=== Compass ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Item
Type: Navigation
Crafted from four iron ingots and one redstone dust. It points to the world spawn
point. A lodestone compass points to a lodestone instead when used on one.

=== Map ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Item
Type: Navigation
Crafted from one paper and four compasses. Maps record explored terrain; an empty map
in hand creates a fresh map of the area. Cartography tables can lock and copy maps.

=== Copper Ore and Blocks ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Item
Type: Resource
Copper ore smelts into copper ingots, used for crafting lightning rods, copper blocks,
and the spyglass. Copper exposed to air gradually oxidizes into a green patina, which
can be waxed with honeycomb to stop the change.

=== Totem of Undying ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Item
Type: Utility
Dropped by evokers in woodland mansions and raids. Holding it in the off-hand prevents
death once, restoring health and granting absorption at the cost of the totem.

=== Obsidian ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Item
Type: Block
How to obtain: Pour water onto a lava source block to create obsidian, then
mine it with a diamond pickaxe; it also generates naturally around lava lakes
deep underground and in ruined portals.
Uses: Nether portal frames (10-14 blocks), enchanting tables (four each),
and blast-resistant building material. It takes a long time to break even
with the best pickaxe.
Where it is found: Everywhere lava meets water — the classic method is a
lava pool with a water bucket.

=== Eyes of Ender ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Item
Type: Material
How to obtain: Craft four ender pearls (from endermen) with four blaze
powder (from blaze rods) to make four eyes of ender.
Uses: Thrown to locate the Stronghold, and placed into the twelve End portal
frame slots to activate it; also used to craft end crystals.
Where it is found: Ender pearls come from endermen or cleric trades; blaze
powder from blazes in Nether fortresses.

=== Elytra ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Item
Type: Utility
What it is: A pair of wings that let you glide and boost through the air —
the only true flight gear in survival.
How to obtain: Taken from the item frame aboard the end ship moored next to
End cities (after defeating the Ender Dragon); not craftable.
Uses: Equip in the chestplate slot, jump from a height and press jump again
to glide, and use firework rockets mid-flight to boost and climb.
Where it is found: End ships only — the single most valuable item in the
game.

=== Firework Rocket ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Item
Type: Utility
How to obtain: Craft paper with gunpowder (one gunpowder plus paper makes
three rockets); no flight-duration ingredient is needed for boosting.
Uses: Boosting elytra flight (use while gliding), celebrating, and powering
dispenser firework launchers.
Where it is found: Paper from sugar cane, gunpowder from creepers, ghasts,
and witches.

=== Emerald ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Item
Type: Currency
What it is: The trading currency of villagers — every villager trade is
priced in emeralds.
How to obtain: Farm and sell crops to farmers, sell sticks to fletchers,
mine emerald ore in mountains, or loot chests.
Uses: Buying enchanted books, diamond gear, maps, potions, and every other
villager service.
Where it is found: Mountain biomes generate emerald ore; villagers and
chest loot provide the rest.

=== Water Bucket ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Item
Type: Tool
How to obtain: Craft three iron ingots into a bucket, then fill it at any
water source.
Uses: MLG water breaks falls, water columns let you climb, lava becomes
obsidian or cobblestone, fires go out, and farm land gets hydrated.
Where it is found: Crafted — keep one in your hotbar at all times; it is the
single best survival tool.

=== Item Stacking ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Game Mechanics
Most items stack to 64. Ender pearls, snowballs, eggs, and end crystals stack to 16.
Tools, weapons, armor, and food with different damage values do not stack together.
