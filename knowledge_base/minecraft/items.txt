# Minecraft — Items

Key items, blocks, and materials in Minecraft.

=== Diamond ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Item
Type: Resource
Diamonds are found only below Y level 16, most often between Y -58 and Y -53, and are
mined with an iron pickaxe or better. They are used for diamond tools, armor, and a
netherite upgrade ingredient via a smithing table.

=== Netherite Ingot ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Item
Type: Resource
Netherite is crafted by combining one netherite scrap with four gold ingots. Netherite
scrap comes from smelting ancient debris, which spawns rarely in the Nether around
Y 8 to Y 22. Netherite tools and armor are stronger than diamond and do not burn in lava.

=== Ender Pearl ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Item
Type: Material
Ender pearls drop from endermen. Throwing one teleports the player a short distance.
Crafting four pearls with four blaze powder creates eyes of ender, which point to the
Stronghold for the End portal.

=== Blaze Rod ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Item
Type: Material
Blaze rods drop from blazes in Nether fortresses. They are fuel for brewing stands and
craft into blaze powder, needed for eyes of ender and to charge an end crystal.

=== Golden Apple ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Item
Type: Food
A golden apple is crafted with eight gold blocks around one apple, or found in chests.
Eating it grants Absorption and Regeneration for a short time, useful in PvP and against
the Wither.

=== Shield ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Item
Type: Tool
Crafted from six planks and one iron ingot. Holding right-click blocks incoming melee
and arrow damage from the front. Banner patterns can be applied to customize shields
in Bedrock Edition.

=== Bow ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Item
Type: Weapon
Crafted from three sticks and three string. Arrows are crafted from flint, a stick, and
a feather. A bow can be enchanted with Power, Infinity, Flame, and Punch.

=== Iron Pickaxe ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Item
Type: Tool
Crafted from three iron ingots and two sticks. It is the minimum tier needed to mine
diamond ore, and it mines most stone blocks quickly.

=== Compass ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Item
Type: Navigation
Crafted from four iron ingots and one redstone dust. It points to the world spawn
point. A lodestone compass points to a lodestone instead when used on one.

=== Map ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Item
Type: Navigation
Crafted from one paper and four compasses. Maps record explored terrain; an empty map
in hand creates a fresh map of the area. Cartography tables can lock and copy maps.

=== Copper Ore and Blocks ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Item
Type: Resource
Copper ore smelts into copper ingots, used for crafting lightning rods, copper blocks,
and the spyglass. Copper exposed to air gradually oxidizes into a green patina, which
can be waxed with honeycomb to stop the change.

=== Totem of Undying ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Item
Type: Utility
Dropped by evokers in woodland mansions and raids. Holding it in the off-hand prevents
death once, restoring health and granting absorption at the cost of the totem.

=== Item Stacking ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Game Mechanics
Most items stack to 64. Ender pearls, snowballs, eggs, and end crystals stack to 16.
Tools, weapons, armor, and food with different damage values do not stack together.
