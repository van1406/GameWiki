# Minecraft — Armor

Armor materials, progression, protection values, durability, and how to obtain
and upgrade every armor set.

=== Armor Pieces ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Armor
Four armor pieces cover the body: helmet (head), chestplate (torso), leggings
(legs), and boots (feet). Each piece is crafted from its material in a
crafting table using the same pattern for every tier — helmet: 5 material in a
U, chestplate: 8 (all but the top-center), leggings: 7, boots: 4. Equip armor
from the inventory or press the armor-slot shortcut to put a full set on.

=== Leather Armor ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Armor
Materials: Leather — 7 pieces craft a full set (helmet 5, chestplate 8,
leggings 7, boots 4 per piece pattern).
How to obtain: Crafted from leather dropped by cows, horses, and llamas; also
found in pillager outposts and fishing loot.
Protection: The weakest armor — a full set blocks around 28% of damage.
Special use: Leather armor can be dyed any color in a crafting grid or cauldron.
Tips: Only worth crafting on day one before any iron is found.

=== Chainmail Armor ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Armor
What it is: A mid-tier armor set that cannot be crafted — chainmail exists
only as loot.
How to obtain: Rare drops from zombies and skeletons, chest loot, and armorer
villager trades.
Protection: Better than leather but weaker than iron.
Durability and repair: It cannot be repaired with a material (there is no
chainmail crafting material), so combine two identical pieces on an anvil or
use Mending to keep it going.
Tips: Treat chainmail as a bonus — wear it until your first full iron set is
ready, then replace it.

=== Iron Armor ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Armor
Materials: 24 iron ingots for a full set (helmet 5, chestplate 8, leggings 7,
boots 4 ingots per piece).
How to obtain: Smelt iron ore (or raw iron) and craft each piece.
Protection: A full set gives 15 armor points — 60% damage reduction.
Tips: Iron armor is the workhorse set: cheap, repairable, and good enough for
the Nether with fire resistance. Get a full set plus a shield before mining
diamonds.

=== Gold Armor ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Armor
Materials: Gold ingots, same patterns as other tiers.
How to obtain: Crafted from gold ingots, found in chests, or traded from
armorer villagers.
Protection: Weak and very low durability — the worst combat armor.
Special use: Wearing any gold armor piece makes piglins and brutes neutral
toward you in the Nether, which is its main purpose.
Tips: Carry one gold piece (boots or a helmet) for Nether trips and wear your
real armor around it.

=== Diamond Armor ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Armor
Materials: 24 diamonds for a full set.
How to obtain: Find diamonds below y 16, then craft each piece in a crafting
table.
Protection: A full set gives 20 armor points — 80% damage reduction — plus
armor toughness that helps against strong hits.
Tips: Enchant the set with Protection, Unbreaking, and Mending before wearing
it into the End. Diamond is the standard endgame-quality gear.

=== Netherite Armor ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Armor
How to obtain: Upgrade each diamond armor piece at a smithing table with one
netherite ingot (four netherite scrap + four gold ingots); modern versions
also need a netherite upgrade smithing template found in bastion remnant
chests.
Protection: Same 20 armor points as diamond but with higher armor toughness,
more durability, and knockback resistance; netherite armor does not burn in
fire or lava.
Tips: Upgrade chestplate and leggings first for the toughness bonus, and keep
the diamond set enchanted — enchantments carry over through the upgrade.

=== Armor Progression ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Armor
What is the armor progression?
1. Leather (or chainmail loot) on day one.
2. Full iron as soon as you smelt iron — the practical mid-game set.
3. Diamond once you return from your first deep mine.
4. Netherite after visiting the Nether and mining ancient debris.
Each step raises armor points and toughness. Boots are usually upgraded last;
the chestplate gives the most overall value.

=== Protection and Armor Toughness ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Armor
How does protection work? Each armor point blocks 4% of incoming damage, so
20 points (full diamond or netherite) blocks 80%. Armor toughness (on diamond
and netherite) reduces damage from strong hits such as creeper explosions and
fall damage.
Enchantments stack with armor: Protection reduces all damage types, while
Fire, Blast, and Projectile Protection specialize against fire/explosions,
creepers and TNT, and arrows. Feather Falling on boots reduces fall damage,
and Thorns reflects a little damage back to attackers.
Tips: A full Protection IV set is the single biggest survivability upgrade in
the game; combine special protections only when you face a known threat such
as the Wither or a ghast-heavy Nether run.

=== Durability and Repair ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Armor
Durability from lowest to highest: leather, gold, chainmail, iron, diamond,
netherite. Armor takes damage when you take damage.
How to repair: On an anvil, combine the damaged piece with its material or an
identical piece; enchanted gear keeps its enchantments. Mending repairs armor
automatically from experience orbs, and Unbreaking slows wear.
Tips: Keep a spare iron set in a chest for Nether trips where lava can end a
run, and put Mending on your best set once you reach villager trading.

=== Getting and Upgrading Armor ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Armor
How do I get better armor quickly?
1. Punch cows for leather or loot chainmail from mobs while gathering wood.
2. Mine iron as soon as you have a stone pickaxe and craft a full iron set.
3. Bring an iron pickaxe, food, and a water bucket down to y -59 branch mines
   for diamonds.
4. Craft the diamond set and enchant it at level 30 with a full bookshelf
   setup.
5. In the Nether, mine ancient debris at y 8 to y 22 and upgrade diamond
   pieces to netherite at a smithing table.
6. Repair everything with Mending and Unbreaking so one set lasts forever.
