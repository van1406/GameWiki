# Minecraft — Game Mechanics

Core gameplay systems of Minecraft: survival, mining, brewing, and more.

=== Health and Hunger ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Game Mechanics
The player has 20 health points (10 hearts) and 20 hunger points. Hunger drains while
sprinting, jumping, and fighting. When hunger is above 18, health regenerates slowly.
When hunger reaches 0, health slowly drains. Eating food restores hunger; cooked food
restores more than raw food.

=== Mining and Tool Tiers ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Game Mechanics
Tool tiers from lowest to highest: wood, stone, iron, diamond, netherite.
Wooden and stone tools mine most basic blocks; iron is required for diamond ore,
diamond for obsidian, and any pickaxe tier works on dirt and wood. Using the wrong tool
drops nothing, for example mining ores with a wooden pickaxe or stone with a wooden axe.

=== Experience and Enchanting ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Game Mechanics
Experience orbs drop from mining ores, killing mobs, and smelting. Levels are spent at
an enchanting table, which requires one book, two diamonds, and four obsidian to craft,
plus lapis lazuli to enchant. Enchantments include Efficiency, Unbreaking, Fortune,
Silk Touch, Sharpness, and Protection. An anvil combines enchanted books and renames
items; a grindstone disenches items for experience back.

=== Brewing Potions ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Game Mechanics
A brewing stand is crafted from one blaze rod and three cobblestone and uses blaze
powder as fuel. The base is a water bottle plus nether wart to make an awkward potion.
Common potions: Healing (glistering melon), Strength (blaze powder), Fire Resistance
(magma cream), Swiftness (sugar), and Night Vision (golden carrot). Gunpowder turns a
potion into a splash potion; dragon's breath makes a lingering potion.

=== The Nether ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Game Mechanics
The Nether is a hell-like dimension reached by building a portal from 10 to 14 obsidian
blocks in a rectangular frame and igniting it with flint and steel. One block in the
Nether equals eight blocks in the Overworld, so Nether travel is fast. Beds explode in
the Nether, and water cannot be placed without a cauldron trick.

=== The End ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Game Mechanics
The End is reached through a Stronghold portal frame filled with twelve eyes of ender.
The dimension contains the Ender Dragon boss and End cities with shulkers, elytra, and
elytra-boosting fireworks. The exit portal appears when the dragon dies.

=== Redstone Basics ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Game Mechanics
Redstone dust transmits power up to 15 blocks from a power source such as a lever,
button, torch, or pressure plate. Repeaters extend and delay the signal, comparators
read container fullness, and pistons push blocks. These components automate farms,
doors, and storage systems.

=== Farming ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Game Mechanics
Crops need farmland created with a hoe on dirt or grass blocks, and water within four
blocks to stay hydrated. Wheat grows from seeds in 5 to 10 minutes with bone meal
instantly. Animals breed when fed their favorite food, producing a baby after a short
delay.

=== Raid Mechanics ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Game Mechanics
Killing a raid captain grants the Bad Omen effect. Entering a village with Bad Omen
starts a raid with waves of pillagers, vindicators, ravagers, and evokers. Winning the
raid grants the Hero of the Village effect, which gives discounted villager trades.

=== Day and Night Cycle ===
Game: Minecraft
Category: Game Mechanics
A full Minecraft day lasts 20 minutes: 10 minutes of daylight, a short sunset and
sunrise, and 7 minutes of night. Sleeping in a bed skips the night. Hostile mobs spawn
in darkness, so lighting up the area at night is important for survival.
